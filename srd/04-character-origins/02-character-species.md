## Character Species

When you choose your character's species on Azhi, there is only one choice: your character is a dragonborn. What remains to choose is which dragonborn they have become.

The dragonborn are the only people of Azhi. They live on the corpse of the great dragon Azhi, beneath the wandering light of the Gold and Silver dragons, and they are the descendants and followers of the seven young dragons of the myth. No other sapient peoples share the world with them.

Every dragonborn is born polychromatic, scales shifting through many colours. As they mature, most settle into a form with an elemental affinity to the five surface dragons, Red, Green, Blue, Black and White, or to a blend of two of them. A dragonborn's form isn't inherited; it comes from the life they lead. Siblings may settle into different colours, and a Red dragonborn raised in a Blue harbour town is unremarkable.

No colour is greater than another. The colours differ as the elements do, and the quarrels between them are disputes over policy, not claims of superiority.

### The Colour Wheel

The forms are arranged around a wheel of colour. Five are the colours of the surface dragons. Four are **blends**, which sit between two neighbouring colours and mix the way paint does: Red and Green together make Brown, and Black and White make Grey. Yellow is the exception: it is Red and Green mixed the way light does, the same parents as Brown, giving daylight instead of earth. Two of the blends, Yellow and Grey, are the closest earthly echoes of the heavenly dragons, Gold the sun and Silver the moon.

Going around the wheel: **Red, Brown, Yellow, Green, Blue, Indigo, Black, Grey, White,** and back to Red. Green and Blue sit side by side with no blend between them, as do White and Red.

**The Colour Wheel**

### Parts of a Form

A form includes the following parts.

#### Size

Your character's form determines the character's size. Blue dragonborn are Small and lithe; Red dragonborn are Large.

#### Speed

Your character's form determines the character's Speed. Any Swim Speed, Climb Speed or Fly Speed a form grants is equal to your Speed.

#### Ability Score Increase

Your form increases one ability score by 1, chosen from those listed. This increase can't raise a score above 20. An Unphased dragonborn has no form and so gains no increase from it.

#### Special Traits

Each form gives a character special traits. When you choose a form, your character gets all the special traits listed for it. Some traits involve making a choice from a handful of options.

#### Ribbons

Every form also has two ribbons: small knacks and habits with little or no effect on the game. They give your character flavour.

### Unphased

**Size:** Small or Medium (chosen when you select this form)
**Speed:** 30 feet

You haven't settled into a form yet. Your scales shift through every colour, and you're still discovering who you are. As an Unphased dragonborn, you have these special traits.

_Many Possibilities._ You choose three personality traits instead of two, and you have no form.

_Trying On Personalities._ When you finish a Long Rest, you can replace one of your personality traits with another one. Your ability scores change to match.

### Forms

The forms are presented in wheel order.

#### Red

_Red dragonborn are the builders of Azhi: miners, crafters, smiths and industrialists, at home with metal and stone on the volcanic mountains. They are big and hardy, but no one who has seen a Red forge at work mistakes them for brutes._

**Size:** Large
**Speed:** 35 feet
**Ability Score Increase:** Strength

As a Red dragonborn, you have these special traits.

_Mighty Frame._ You have Advantage on Strength checks.

_Fire Resistance._ You have Resistance to Fire damage.

_Maker's Training._ You have proficiency with two tools of your choice, and in your choice of the Athletics or Intimidation skill. Choose one of those tools; you have Expertise with it.

_Ribbons._ At a glance, you can judge the quality and origin of anything of value. _Prospector:_ you can make Wisdom (Survival) checks to find raw materials for crafting, such as ore, workable stone, clay or fuel.

#### Brown (Red + Green)

_Brown dragonborn work the rich ash-fed earth: farmers, woodcutters and fringe-dwelling semi-industrialists, and the occasional hermit. They are burly folk, used to the scythe and the axe, the plough-ox and the laden cart._

**Size:** Medium
**Speed:** 30 feet
**Ability Score Increase:** Constitution or Strength

As a Brown dragonborn, you have these special traits.

_Rooted._ While you're standing on the ground, you have Advantage on saving throws and ability checks against being pushed, pulled or knocked Prone.

_Tremorsense._ You have Tremorsense with a range of 10 feet.

_Plough and Cart._ You can control any willing Beast at least one size larger than you as a mount, even if it hasn't been trained to accept a rider. You have Advantage on ability checks to drive a vehicle and to keep a mount under control.

_Brown Folk._ You have proficiency in two of the following skills of your choice: Animal Handling, Nature, Perception or Survival. You also have proficiency with one of the following tools of your choice: Carpenter's Tools, Cook's Utensils or Woodcarver's Tools.

_Ribbons._ _Sky Sense:_ you can predict the weather, and when light will come and go, over the next day. _Wisdom of the Almanac:_ you've read and absorbed the farmers' almanac, the collected wisdom shared and published by farmers, and can quote it for any occasion. Its accuracy is never questioned, though it probably should be.

#### Yellow (Red + Green)

_Yellow dragonborn are the bright sparks of Azhi: inventors lit by flashes of inspiration, as sudden as lightning and as warm as sunlight. They give every discovery away to anyone who asks, they are hopeless at business, and they are the closest earthly echo of Gold, the sun._

**Size:** Medium
**Speed:** 30 feet
**Ability Score Increase:** Intelligence or Constitution

As a Yellow dragonborn, you have these special traits.

_Lightning Resistance._ You have Resistance to Lightning damage.

_Bright Idea._ When you, or a willing creature within 30 feet of you that can see or hear you, fail an ability check using a skill you're proficient in, you can change the d20 roll to a 20. Once you use this trait, you can't use it again until you finish a Long Rest.

_Sunspark._ You know the _Light_ cantrip, and you can cast it without a Material component.

_Yellow Training._ You have proficiency in two of the following skills of your choice: Arcana, Investigation, Nature or Performance. You also have proficiency with one tool of your choice.

_Ribbons._ _Usual Suspect:_ when something nearby explodes, burns or mysteriously breaks, people assume it was you. _Impractical Inventions:_ you always have some odd contraption on you, like a self-stirring spoon that only spins backwards. They never quite do what you advertised, or anything useful at all.

#### Green

_Green dragonborn are at home in the canopy. They are hunters, wardens and scouts of dense, overgrown country, as sure on a swaying branch as on the ground, and often found in high places, watching._

**Size:** Medium
**Speed:** 30 feet
**Ability Score Increase:** Dexterity or Constitution

As a Green dragonborn, you have these special traits.

_Climber._ You have a Climb Speed equal to your Speed.

_Prehensile Tail._ Your tail counts as a free third hand. It can hold and manipulate objects as a hand can, but it can't attack with a weapon or gain the benefit of a Shield. You can make Unarmed Strikes with it, dealing 1d4 Slashing damage and using Dexterity for the attack and damage rolls.

_Green Lore._ You have proficiency in the Nature and Survival skills, and with your choice of the Herbalism Kit or the Poisoner's Kit.

_Ribbons._ By touching a plant, you can tell whether it's safe to eat. _Beds Are Overrated:_ you can sleep anywhere, including hanging by your tail from a tree branch.

#### Blue

_Blue dragonborn are the people of the coasts: sailors and pirates, traders and merchants, at home wherever water carries goods. They welcome everyone, and the port cities they run are the great cosmopolitan crossroads of Azhi._

**Size:** Small
**Speed:** 30 feet
**Ability Score Increase:** Dexterity or Charisma

As a Blue dragonborn, you have these special traits.

_Amphibious._ You can breathe air and water, and you have a Swim Speed equal to your Speed.

_Slippery._ You have Advantage on ability checks and saving throws you make to escape the Grappled or Restrained condition.

_Trader's Tongue._ When you fail a Charisma check, you can reroll it, and you must use the new roll. Once you use this trait, you can't use it again until you finish a Long Rest.

_Trader's Training._ You have proficiency in the Insight and Persuasion skills and with Navigator's Tools.

_Ribbons._ You always know which way the nearest water lies. _A Word in Every Port:_ you can identify any language you hear by its sounds alone, and when someone speaks a second language, you can place their accent and tell where they learned it. You know a word or two of each.

#### Indigo (Blue + Black)

_Indigo dragonborn dwell where the sea goes dark, in cities few outsiders have ever seen. They surface in the port towns dealing in secrets, debts, dangers and forbidden things, and they use what they trade: theirs are the spies and the assassins. Most people know civilization needs someone to do what Indigo do, and are quietly glad they do it, but few trust them. Every favour an Indigo wins ashore buys standing in the deeps below._

**Size:** Medium
**Speed:** 30 feet
**Ability Score Increase:** Charisma

As an Indigo dragonborn, you have these special traits.

_Deep-Born._ You can breathe air and water, and you have a Swim Speed equal to your Speed.

_Darkvision._ You have Darkvision with a range of 60 feet.

_Black Pearls._ Unless you've recently phased into Indigo, you start with two black pearls, the tokens of debt traded by the Undertow. If you've recently phased into Indigo, you will be approached shortly with an opportunity. For the Undertow itself, see "Factions."

_Deep Training._ You have proficiency in two of the following skills of your choice: Deception, Insight, Intimidation, Persuasion or Stealth.

_Ribbons._ _Knowledge Is Power, and Power Is Knowledge:_ you have an innate feeling for which pieces of knowledge are important to keep or to share, and for the opportune moment to do either. _Necessary Evil:_ everyone knows what your people do, and most are quietly glad someone does. Even so, when you walk into a room, the sensitive conversations stop.

#### Black

_Black dragonborn prize knowledge. Some keep it for its own sake, as record keepers, librarians and archaeologists; others use it as leverage, as the Black dragon once sought worshippers. Theirs is a living god, and many are fervent in its worship. Most live underground._

**Size:** Medium
**Speed:** 30 feet
**Ability Score Increase:** Intelligence or Charisma

As a Black dragonborn, you have these special traits.

_Darkvision._ You have Darkvision with a range of 120 feet. Of all the dragonborn, only Black and Indigo dragonborn have Darkvision.

_Guarded Mind._ You have Advantage on Intelligence, Wisdom and Charisma saving throws.

_Scholar's Rituals._ You can cast _Comprehend Languages_ and _Identify_, but only as Rituals, and without Material components.

_Black Lore._ You have proficiency in two of the following skills of your choice: Arcana, Deception, History, Persuasion or Religion.

_Ribbons._ _Bone Reader:_ you can tell whether bone, including the world's own, is natural or worked, and roughly how old it is. From its anatomy, you can also tell where in Azhi's body you are and which way you're facing. _True Convictions:_ you can see through false faiths, false prophets and invented gods, and you always know your own convictions.

#### Grey (Black + White)

_Grey dragonborn put Black's knowledge to work for others: investigators and detectives, wandering sages who notice what everyone else misses, though a few turn the same skills to burglary. Theirs is the quiet, watchful light of the moon, the closest earthly echo of Silver._

**Size:** Medium
**Speed:** 30 feet
**Ability Score Increase:** Intelligence or Wisdom

As a Grey dragonborn, you have these special traits.

_Keen Investigator._ You can take the Search or Study action as a Bonus Action. You can also cast _Augury_, _Detect Magic_ and _Find Traps_, but only as Rituals and without Material components; for you, _Find Traps_ has the Ritual tag.

_Moonsight._ Within 60 feet of you, you can see in Dim Light as if it were Bright Light.

_Heard It on the Road._ When you arrive in a settlement, you know its local customs, who's in charge, and a rumour or two.

_Grey Training._ You have proficiency in two of the following skills of your choice: History, Insight, Investigation, Perception or Religion. You also have proficiency with one of the following tools of your choice: Calligrapher's Supplies, Cartographer's Tools, Forgery Kit or Thieves' Tools.

_Ribbons._ _Uncanny Timekeeping:_ you always know the exact time, even underground or with no sky to read. _Air of Authority:_ when things go sideways, people uncannily assume you're the right person to talk to.

#### White

_White dragonborn are the most empathetic of the colours: healers, storytellers and keepers of oral tradition, who travel from village to village mending what is broken. They gather in monasteries hidden in the cold mountains, and they fly to rise above the chaos below, but they land because they care._

**Size:** Medium
**Speed:** 30 feet
**Ability Score Increase:** Wisdom

As a White dragonborn, you have these special traits.

_Cold Resistance._ You have Resistance to Cold damage.

_Helping Hand._ You can take the Help action as a Bonus Action.

_Spectral Wings._ As a Bonus Action, you can summon wings of shimmering light and gain a Fly Speed equal to your Speed for a number of minutes equal to your character level. The wings fade when the duration ends. Once you use this trait, you can't use it again until you finish a Long Rest.

_Healer's Training._ You have proficiency in the Medicine skill.

_Ribbons._ You're very good at memorizing short messages and keeping them, so you can serve as a courier. _Apocryphal Sayings:_ is it wisdom, or just rote repetition? Either way, you seem to have an endless supply for the opportune moment.

### Gold and Silver Dragonborn

Gold and Silver dragonborn are extremely rare. The Gold and Silver dragons, who are the sun and moon, generally take no direct interest in the activities of those below, and there is no easy way for a character to settle or phase into either colour. Gold and Silver dragonborn appear as nonplayer characters who can drive a story. Yellow and Grey dragonborn are their closest earthly echoes.

### Settling and Phasing

_Settling._ What makes a young dragonborn settle into a form is left to your character's story: finding a calling, as the young dragons of the myth did, the pull of their surroundings, or anything else you invent.

_Phasing._ Something dramatic, traumatic or deadly can make a dragonborn phase from one form into another. They keep parts of their former personality and most of their memories. Rebirth runs deep on Azhi, where everything the great dragon devoured "springs forth anew." Phasing carries a penalty, so it can't be used for advantage. _(OPEN: the penalty isn't yet designed, nor whether a dragonborn's first settling counts as phasing.)_

_A New Society._ A dragonborn who phases must find a place among their new colour. Some stay in their old role under a new colour; others seek out their new people and start again, often at the bottom of the ladder. An Indigo newcomer, for one, arrives with no name and no pearls.

_No Return._ Once settled, a dragonborn can't return to being Unphased. The DM may allow it on rare occasions, for the sake of the story.
