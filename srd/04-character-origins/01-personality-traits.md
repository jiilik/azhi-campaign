# Character Origins

Every person on Azhi is a dragonborn. Your character's origin is the dragonborn they have become: the form they have settled into, if any, and the personality traits that shaped them along the way. Together, these replace the backgrounds and species of the core rules.

An origin has two parts:

- **Form.** Your character's colour, or the lack of one. It determines your character's creature type, size, Speed, one ability score increase and special traits. See "Character Species."
- **Personality Traits.** Two traits that describe who your character is, each with an ability score increase and a small benefit. An Unphased character has three. See "Personality Traits" below.

Between them, your form and personality traits give your character the ability score increases, skill proficiencies and tool proficiencies that a background would otherwise provide.

**Starting Equipment.** Because there are no backgrounds, your character starts with 50 GP to spend on equipment, in addition to the equipment from their class.

## Personality Traits

Your character's personality traits describe who they are: the habits, quirks and passions that were most formative before they embarked on a life of adventure. On Azhi, personality is also what guides a dragonborn toward the form they settle into, just as the young dragons of the myth found their forms by finding what they loved.

### Parts of a Personality Trait

The description of a personality trait contains the following parts, which are presented after the trait's name.

_Ability Score Increase._ Each trait lists one or two abilities. Increase one of them by 1. This increase can't raise a score above 20. A trait that lists only one ability (marked ★ in the table) has a slightly stronger benefit, since it offers less choice.

_Benefit._ The benefits of a trait are detailed after its ability score increase. Some benefits give proficiency in a skill or a tool. Tools are detailed in "Equipment."

### Choosing Personality Traits

A phased dragonborn chooses two personality traits. An Unphased dragonborn chooses three, and can change one of them on each Long Rest (see "Unphased" in "Character Species"). You can't choose the same trait twice.

Personality traits aren't tied to colours; any character can choose any trait. Some traits pair naturally with certain forms, and some pairings are especially strong. Your two traits and your form may all raise the same ability, though no score can go above 20.

**Personality Traits by Ability**

| Ability | Traits |
|---|---|
| Strength | The Bold, The Commander, The Disciplined, The Gentle Giant, The Guardian, The Rock, The Scrapper |
| Dexterity | The Bold, The Curious, The Magnificent, The Resourceful, The Restless ★, The Scrapper, The Wanderer |
| Constitution | The Disciplined, The Generous, The Guardian, The Hearty ★, The Rock, The Steward, The Wanderer |
| Intelligence | The Curious, The Debater, The Prudent, The Steward, The Strategist ★, The Theorist ★ |
| Wisdom | The Confidant, The Devout ★, The Dreamer ★, The Gentle Giant, The Mentor, The Prudent, The Resourceful |
| Charisma | The Commander, The Confidant, The Debater, The Generous, The Magnificent, The Mentor, The Unbound ★ |

### Personality Trait Descriptions

The traits are alphabetized, ignoring "The."

#### The Bold

_You were halfway across the chasm before anyone finished saying "we should think about this." Danger doesn't frighten you; it wakes you up. Half your stories end with "and then it got worse," and you tell every one of them grinning._

**Ability Score Increase:** Strength or Dexterity

You gain the following benefits.

_Quick to Act†._ When you roll Initiative, add your Proficiency Bonus to the roll.

_First Through the Door._ When you volunteer to go first into something risky, such as a jump, a duel or a confrontation, you have Advantage on the first ability check you make for it.

#### The Commander

_When the shouting starts, every head turns to you, and you've already decided. You see where the group should be going and can't help pointing the way. You'd rather make a bold call and own it than wait for a perfect one, and somehow people keep following you._

**Ability Score Increase:** Strength or Charisma

You gain the following benefits.

_Proficiency._ You gain proficiency in your choice of the Intimidation or Persuasion skill.

_Follow Me._ Allies within 30 feet of you who can see or hear you have Advantage on saving throws they make to avoid or end the Frightened condition.

_Chain of Command._ Soldiers, guards and militia recognize you as one of their own. You can always get a hearing with whoever is in charge of them.

#### The Confidant

_You notice the pause before an answer, the glance toward the door, the smile that stops short of the eyes. People tell you things they've told no one else, and you often know how a story ends before it's half told. It isn't magic. You simply pay attention._

**Ability Score Increase:** Wisdom or Charisma

You gain the following benefits.

_Proficiency._ You gain proficiency in the Insight skill.

_Keeper of Confidences._ When a person, place or faction comes up, you can make an Intelligence check to recall something someone once told you about it in confidence, such as a secret, a rumour or a quiet grudge. The DM supplies the detail.

_Unspoken Signals._ You know the _Message_ cantrip. For you it takes no whisper, just a raised eyebrow or a tap of the nose that your companions understand.

#### The Curious

_A locked door is a question, and you've never once left a question alone. You read the margins, pull the lever marked "do not pull", and take the left fork, then come back for the right one. Your companions have learned to count heads before leaving a room._

**Ability Score Increase:** Dexterity or Intelligence

You gain the following benefits.

_Proficiencies._ You gain proficiency in two skills or tools of your choice.

_Nose for Secrets†._ You have Advantage on ability checks you make to find traps and secret doors.

_Poke It and See._ The first time you hit a creature with an attack roll, you learn one of its damage Resistances, Immunities or Vulnerabilities, if it has any.

#### The Debater

_You'll argue that the Gold dragon is just a very bright moth, simply to see who can prove you wrong. Every plan, rule and sacred truth deserves a good poke, and you'll switch sides mid-argument for the fun of it. Nothing delights you more than the rare day someone finally wins one against you._

**Ability Score Increase:** Charisma or Intelligence

You gain the following benefits.

_Proficiencies._ You gain proficiency in the Deception and Persuasion skills.

_Riposte._ When you fail a Charisma (Persuasion) or Charisma (Deception) check, you can immediately try again using the other skill, switching from honest argument to clever spin, or back. You must use the new roll.

_Banter._ As a Bonus Action, you trade quips with a creature that can hear you and understands a language you speak. Make a Charisma (Persuasion or Deception) check contested by its Wisdom (Insight) check. If you win, it's too busy thinking of a comeback to take Reactions until the start of your next turn.

#### The Devout

_You know the old story by heart, and you know the dragons who lived it still fly overhead and walk the land. Your faith isn't a comfort kept for hard nights; it's the reason you set out at all. Wherever the road leads, you walk it as an offering._

**Ability Score Increase:** Wisdom

You gain the following benefits.

_Proficiency._ You gain proficiency in your choice of the Arcana, History or Religion skill.

_Thaumaturgy._ You know the _Thaumaturgy_ cantrip. Wisdom is your spellcasting ability for it.

_Steadfast Faith._ When you, or an ally you can see within 30 feet of you, make a Death Saving Throw, add your Wisdom modifier to the roll.

_Faithful Welcome._ At any shrine or temple of your faith, you and your companions can find free food, lodging and basic healing, and the clergy will hear you out.

#### The Disciplined

_You're up before the light, running the same drills you ran yesterday, only a little better. Every scar is a lesson, every sore muscle a promise kept. Others see a routine; you see a path with no end, and you mean to walk every step of it._

**Ability Score Increase:** Strength or Constitution

You gain the following benefits.

_Proficiency._ You gain proficiency in one skill or tool of your choice.

_Muscle Memory._ Choose one skill or tool you're proficient in. Whenever you make an ability check with it, roll a d6 and add the number rolled. The die becomes a d8 at character level 5, a d10 at level 11 and a d12 at level 17. To change your chosen skill or tool, you must practise the new one for seven Long Rests in a row.

#### The Dreamer

_Somewhere behind your eyes is a better world, and you can see it in every detail. You hold to what's right even when it costs you, and you'd rather fail at something worth doing than succeed at something hollow. People mistake your quiet for distance. It's conviction._

**Ability Score Increase:** Wisdom

You gain the following benefits.

_Proficiency._ You gain proficiency in one of the following of your choice: the Animal Handling or Insight skill, Calligrapher's Supplies or Painter's Supplies.

_Prophetic Dreams._ Before a Long Rest, you can ask the DM a question about something that troubles you. You dream an answer that's cryptic, symbolic and never wrong, but always open to interpretation.

_Déjà Vu._ You can act on a half-remembered dream: either use your Reaction to impose Disadvantage on an attack roll against you, or give yourself Advantage on an attack roll you make. Once you use this benefit, you can't do so again until you finish a Short or Long Rest.

#### The Generous

_Your door is never barred and your pot is never empty. You learn every name, and you make sure the quiet one at the edge of the fire gets the warmest seat. Coin is for spending and food is for sharing, and somehow you always seem to have enough of both._

**Ability Score Increase:** Charisma or Constitution

You gain the following benefits.

_Guidance._ You know the _Guidance_ cantrip.

_After You._ When an ally you can see within 30 feet of you would take a Reaction but has already used theirs, you can spend your Reaction instead to let them take it.

_The Shirt off Your Back._ When you spend Hit Point Dice during a Short Rest, you can give any of them to an ally instead, who rolls them and regains Hit Points.

#### The Gentle Giant

_You could lift a cart one-handed, so you've learned to be careful with everything else. You hold eggs, kittens and other people's feelings like they might break, because they might. Folk fear you until they meet you; then they ask you to carry their children on your shoulders._

**Ability Score Increase:** Strength or Wisdom

You gain the following benefits.

_Proficiency._ You gain proficiency in your choice of the Animal Handling, Athletics, Intimidation or Medicine skill.

_Soothing Presence._ You can take an action to touch a willing creature and end the Charmed or Frightened condition on it.

_Booming Voice._ Whenever you choose, your voice carries as if amplified. When you use it to make a Charisma (Intimidation) check against a group, you roll once for all of them.

#### The Guardian

_You remember who takes their tea bitter and who flinches at thunder, and you quietly make room for both. You're the last to eat and the first to stand between your friends and whatever's coming. Nobody asked you to keep them safe; you simply never stopped._

**Ability Score Increase:** Constitution or Strength

You gain the following benefits.

_Shield Bearer._ When an ally within 5 feet of you takes damage, you can use your Reaction to take half of that damage instead.

_Shield Training._ You gain training with Shields. If you already have it, you instead gain a +1 bonus to Armor Class while you wield a Shield.

#### The Hearty

_You'll eat anything once and most things twice. You laugh from the belly, sleep through storms and shrug off the fever that flattens everyone else. Healers find you deeply boring, and you take that as a compliment._

**Ability Score Increase:** Constitution

You gain the following benefits.

_Iron Stomach†._ You have Resistance to Poison damage, and you have Advantage on saving throws you make to avoid or end the Poisoned condition.

_Hardy†._ Your Hit Point maximum increases by 1, and it increases by 1 again whenever you gain a level.

#### The Magnificent

_When you walk into a room, the music seems to get louder. You laugh easily, dress brightly and make strangers feel like old friends, and crowds have a way of drifting toward you. You never ask for the spotlight. It just keeps finding you._

**Ability Score Increase:** Charisma or Dexterity

You gain the following benefits.

_Proficiency._ You gain proficiency in one of the following of your choice: the Acrobatics, Performance or Sleight of Hand skill, one musical instrument, the Disguise Kit or Painter's Supplies.

_Prestidigitation._ You know the _Prestidigitation_ cantrip. Charisma is your spellcasting ability for it.

_All Eyes on Me._ While you perform where others can see you, your allies have Advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks to slip past anyone watching you.

_Life of the Party._ At any festival, tavern or other gathering, you're soon known and liked, and people seek you out to talk, share news or buy you a drink.

#### The Mentor

_You see what someone could become long before they do, and you can't help nudging them toward it. Your praise lands like a gift, and even your hard truths somehow land kindly. Half the people you've met are better for knowing you; the other half just haven't noticed yet._

**Ability Score Increase:** Charisma or Wisdom

You gain the following benefits.

_Proficiency._ You gain proficiency in one skill or tool of your choice.

_Lessons._ When you finish a Long Rest, choose one ally who spent time learning from you. Until your next Long Rest, they have proficiency in one skill or tool that you're proficient in.

_Teachable Moment._ When an ally you can see misses with an attack roll, you can use your Reaction to give them Advantage on their next attack roll before the end of their next turn.

#### The Prudent

_You've already thought about what could go wrong, and you've packed for most of it. When the bridge gives way and everyone else is shouting, you're handing out the rope you brought for exactly this. It isn't luck, and it isn't prophecy: just a careful eye and a lifelong habit of asking "and then what?"_

**Ability Score Increase:** Wisdom or Intelligence

You gain the following benefits.

_Proficiency._ You gain proficiency in your choice of the History, Investigation or Perception skill.

_Packed for It._ When you buy equipment, you can prepay any amount of money for unspecified gear. Later, you can produce an ordinary item that could have fit in your pack, and its cost comes off what you prepaid.

_Read Their Intent._ As a Bonus Action, make a Wisdom (Insight) check against a creature you can see. On a success, the DM tells you what it means to do on its next turn, and you can warn your allies.

#### The Resourceful

_You never pack the right thing, and you never need to. A belt buckle becomes a lockpick, a cloak becomes a stretcher, and a hopeless mess becomes something you can work with. When the plan falls apart, you're already rolling up your sleeves._

**Ability Score Increase:** Wisdom or Dexterity

You gain the following benefits.

_Proficiencies._ You gain proficiency in your choice of the Medicine, Nature, Sleight of Hand or Survival skill, and with your choice of the Herbalism Kit, Tinker's Tools or Woodcarver's Tools.

_Improvised Tools._ When you lack a tool you're proficient with, you can cobble one together from what's around and use it with no penalty.

_Make Do._ You have proficiency with improvised weapons, and you can use any object you're holding as a Spellcasting Focus. Costly components are still required.

#### The Restless

_Sitting still feels like a punishment. Your fingers drum, your foot taps, and if there's nothing to do you'll find something: whittle a stick, reorganize the packs, pace a groove in the floor. Kings have lost their thrones for less, but you'd rather be doing something than ruling anything._

**Ability Score Increase:** Dexterity

You gain the following benefits.

_Proficiencies._ You gain proficiency with two tools of your choice.

_Catnap._ You can finish a Short Rest in 10 minutes instead of 1 hour. Once you do so, you can't do so again until you finish a Long Rest.

_Up and At 'Em._ Standing up from the Prone condition costs you no movement.

_Quick Hands._ You can draw or stow a weapon, or take an item out of your pack, without using your one free object interaction.

#### The Rock

_The sky can go dark at noon and the ground can split at your feet, and your hands stay steady. Panic slides off you like rain off scales. In the worst moments, people stop watching the danger and start watching you, because you're the one who hasn't moved._

**Ability Score Increase:** Constitution or Strength

You gain the following benefits.

_Unflappable†._ You can't be Surprised.

_Steady Hands._ You have Advantage on Constitution saving throws you make to maintain Concentration.

_Hold Your Ground._ Your Opportunity Attacks have Advantage.

_Cool Under Pressure._ Being within 5 feet of an enemy doesn't impose Disadvantage on your ranged attack rolls, and you can make Opportunity Attacks with ranged weapons.

#### The Scrapper

_You've never met a fight you didn't like, or one you'd walk away from. A shove in a tavern, a spilled drink, a rival's raised eyebrow: it's all an invitation. You win most, lose some, and buy the drinks either way._

**Ability Score Increase:** Strength or Dexterity

You gain the following benefits.

_Proficiencies._ You gain proficiency in your choice of the Acrobatics, Athletics or Intimidation skill, and you have proficiency with improvised weapons.

_Brawler†._ Your Unarmed Strikes deal Bludgeoning damage equal to 1d4 plus your Strength modifier.

_No Hard Feelings._ After a fair, non-lethal fight with someone, win or lose, they treat you as a friend afterwards.

#### The Steward

_Someone else has the grand idea; you're the one who works out how many mules it needs. You keep the ledgers, draw up the watch rota and know who's slacking before they do. Under your eye, a mad plan becomes a schedule, and a schedule becomes a victory._

**Ability Score Increase:** Intelligence or Constitution

You gain the following benefits.

_Better Help._ You can take the Help action to assist with an ability check even if you lack proficiency in the skill or tool, as long as the ally making the check has it.

_Delegate._ When you're part of a group check, you can choose one participant to roll for the whole group, and their result counts for everyone.

_Ready Assistance†._ When an ally makes an attack roll or ability check, you can use your Reaction before they roll to take the Help action for them.

#### The Strategist

_You see the whole board. Before the first blow lands, you've counted the exits, weighed your enemies and guessed their next three moves, and you're already thinking about the war after this battle. Others call it overthinking; you call it winning._

**Ability Score Increase:** Intelligence

You gain the following benefits.

_Proficiencies._ You gain proficiency in two of the following skills of your choice: Arcana, History, Nature, Religion or Survival.

_Calculated Start†._ When you roll Initiative, you can use your Intelligence modifier instead of your Dexterity modifier.

_Initiative Swap._ Immediately after you roll Initiative, you can swap your Initiative with that of one willing ally in the same combat.

#### The Theorist

_Everyone else accepts that the spine of the world never bends. You want to know why. You chase an idea down to its bones, and you'll happily miss supper doing it. You've read about almost everything and done almost none of it, which has never once stopped you from having a plan._

**Ability Score Increase:** Intelligence

You gain the following benefits.

_Proficiencies._ You gain proficiency in two of the following skills of your choice: Arcana, History, Investigation, Nature or Religion.

_Harebrained Scheme._ When an ally who can hear you is about to make a D20 Test, you can use your Reaction to add your Intelligence modifier to the roll. You can do so a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.

#### The Unbound

_Rules are other people's ideas, and nobody asked you before they made them. You go where you like and bow to no one, not out of spite, but because it never occurred to you to ask permission. Customs, titles and "that's just how it's done" roll right off you, and you're happiest where no one has written the rules yet._

**Ability Score Increase:** Charisma

You gain the following benefits.

_Proficiencies._ You gain proficiency in two of the following of your choice: the Deception, Intimidation, Performance or Persuasion skill, the Disguise Kit, the Forgery Kit, Navigator's Tools, Thieves' Tools or one Gaming Set.

_Free Will†._ You have Advantage on saving throws you make to avoid or end the Charmed condition.

_Closed Mind._ The _Zone of Truth_ spell doesn't affect you, and your thoughts can't be read by magic, such as the _Detect Thoughts_ spell.

#### The Wanderer

_On a world shaped like a ring, every road eventually leads back home, and you mean to walk them all. Three nights under one roof makes you restless, and you'd trade a soft bed for a new horizon without a second thought. Somewhere out there is a view no one has ever drawn, and it's waiting for you._

**Ability Score Increase:** Constitution or Dexterity

You gain the following benefits.

_Proficiencies._ You gain proficiency in two of the following of your choice: the Athletics, Nature, Perception or Survival skill, Cartographer's Tools or Navigator's Tools.

_Long Stride†._ Your Speed increases by 5 feet.

_Pathfinder._ You can't become lost by non-magical means.
