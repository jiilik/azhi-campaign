# Azhi — Setting Bible

The single source of truth for the setting. Every fact carries a status:

- **CANON**: stated or approved by the author (Troy). Do not contradict it.
- **PROPOSED**: suggested by Claude and not yet confirmed. You may build on it, but flag it, and ask before you treat it as settled.
- **OPEN**: a question that still needs a decision. These also appear in `PLAN.md` and `canon/decisions.md`.

When a PROPOSED item is confirmed or rejected, update its status here and add a line to `canon/decisions.md`.

---

## 1. Premise

- **CANON.** The world is a torus (ring), the corpse of Azhi, the ancient "philosopher" space dragon. Azhi ate itself to death, ouroboros-style. Its jaws remain closed on its own tail.
- **CANON.** The founding myth is `canon/azhi-myth.md`. It is in-universe scripture, and its wording is fixed.
- **CANON.** A real torus would collapse into a sphere. Here, Azhi's **spine has infinite stiffness**, which holds the ring's shape. Everything else should feel governed by real physics. The sun–moon cycle is the deliberate exception.
- **CANON.** For worldbuilding, the myth is **true**: it is what actually happened, and the setting is built on it.
- **CANON.** In-universe, the myth is **not the only telling**. Some groups have built their own versions of the story, so characters may hold differing accounts.
- **PROPOSED.** Ideas for variant tellings: a version spread by Black's followers that flatters Black's role; Gold and Silver traditions that omit the hunger entirely; a "finish the meal" cult telling that treats Azhi's hunger as holy.

## 2. The Seven Dragons

- **CANON.** All seven young dragons from the myth are **still alive and active**: Gold, Silver, Black, Blue, White, Red, Green.
- **CANON.** **Gold and Silver** act as the **sun and moon**. They are real dragons flying real paths, so day and night are **completely unpredictable**. This is even more true on a torus.
- **CANON.** The other five have **lairs on the surface** that reflect their elements:

| Dragon | Domain (from the myth) | Lair (CANON: exists; PROPOSED: details) |
|---|---|---|
| Blue | Water ("fills the world with water") | PROPOSED: a sea or river lair |
| White | Ice ("freezes parts of the world into ice") | PROPOSED: glaciers on the spine peaks |
| Red | Fire / mountains ("lavas… casts them into mountains") | PROPOSED: volcanic range |
| Green | Life ("from it springs forth life") | PROPOSED: jungle or forest |
| Black | Knowledge, awareness, worship ("knowledge of us, so that we may be worshipped") | PROPOSED: archives or a hidden lair |

- **CANON (myth).** Black's gift let mortals "control fire, water, earth and ice." This is the origin of magic.
- **PROPOSED.** Black's motive ("so that we may be worshipped") is ambiguous. Black may have been closer to Azhi's accomplice than its pupil.
- **OPEN.** What each of the five surface dragons wants now, as a living god with an agenda.
- **PROPOSED.** A cult that wants to "finish the meal", and pilgrims to the Bite, where Azhi first felt pain.

## 3. Peoples

- **CANON.** The **dragonborn** are the only playable species. They come in the **seven colours** of the dragons.
- **CANON.** There are **no other sentient species**. Any art or text that shows people shows only dragonborn. The dragons themselves (gods) and non-sentient creatures are the only exceptions.
- **CANON.** Each colour's dragonborn are descendants or followers of their dragon and generally share its elemental affinity.
- **CANON. Avoid the "racist dragons" trope.** Differences between colours are **elements in opposition**, not claims of superiority. Do not write NPCs whose defining trait is prejudice against other colours.
- **CANON. Affinity is not inherited.** It comes from the life a dragonborn leads (see *Unphased, Phased and phasing* below). A Red dragonborn raised in a Blue harbour town is unremarkable, and siblings may be different colours. *(This was PROPOSED; it now follows from the phasing lore, 2026-10-20.)*

### Unphased, Phased and phasing

- **CANON.** All dragonborn are **born polychromatic**. Young dragonborn with no settled form are **Unphased**. This mirrors the myth: the young dragons are "of many forms and colours" until each finds what it loves and "their forms become fixed."
- **CANON.** As they mature, dragonborn **settle into a final form** with an elemental affinity to **one or two** of the five surface dragons (Blue, Green, Red, Black, White):
  - **Monochrome:** one colour, giving 5 forms.
  - **Bichrome:** two colours that are **adjacent on the colour wheel**, giving 5 forms, for **10 common forms** in all. Blends are named like mixed paint pigments; for example, Green + Red is **brown**. **Order is irrelevant** (Green + Red is the same form as Red + Green).
- **CANON.** What triggers settling is **left to players' backstories**. It can be finding a calling (the myth's model), the influence of surroundings, or anything else they invent.
- **CANON. Phasing:** something dramatic, traumatic or deadly can make a dragonborn **phase** from one form into another. They keep parts of their former personality and most of their memories. Rebirth and reincarnation are strong themes, echoing the myth, in which everything Azhi devoured "springs forth anew." Phasing **carries a penalty** so that players can't exploit it; the penalty is not yet designed.
- **CANON.** There is **no return to Unphased** within the mechanics. The DM may allow it on rare occasions for narrative reasons.
- **CANON. Gold and Silver dragonborn** are **super rare**. They are NPCs who can motivate plot, and there is no easy way for players to phase into them. Their species definitions should still exist, in case they become relevant. Reason (Troy): the Gold and Silver dragons generally don't take direct interest in the activities of those below.
- **CANON (game design).** **Power budget: Unphased < bichrome < monochrome.**
  - **Unphased** characters are playable. They are young and flexible: they take **three personality traits and no affinity**, and may **swap one trait on each Long Rest** (2026-10-21). Their total bonuses are lower, to encourage players to find a phase and settle.
  - **Bichrome** characters have **their own signature feature** that blends both parents, plus small picks from each (2026-10-21; this replaces "a couple of features from either parent colour"). Their total sits between Unphased and monochrome, to discourage munchkin builds.
- **CANON (2026-10-21). The wheel's adjacency follows the feeding cycle** below: Blue–Green, Green–Red, Red–Black, Black–White, White–Blue. The forbidden pairs are exactly the **checking (opposition) pairs**, so no dragonborn holds two opposed elements.
- **CANON (2026-10-21). Bichrome names:** **Marsh** (Blue + Green, teal), **Soil** (Green + Red, brown/umber), **Oxblood** (Red + Black), **Slate** (Black + White), **Glacier** (White + Blue). The hex colours used in art are working values.
- **PROPOSED. Phasing penalty levers:**
  - The result is set by the event or rolled, not chosen. The SRD *Reincarnate* spell is a model.
  - A fading penalty, like *Raise Dead*'s, or Exhaustion.
  - A cost in memories, relationships or proficiencies.
  - Phasing happens only at the DM's discretion or at death's door.
- **OPEN.**
  - Whether a dragonborn's first settling counts as "phasing" (the word may cover only later changes).
  - What an Unphased dragonborn looks like (the colour-wheel art shows a shifting blend of all five colours), and whether it can breathe or resist anything.
  - Whether breath weapons are shared by every dragonborn or belong to particular forms.
  - The exact phasing penalty.
  - Whether Gold and Silver can ever be reached by phasing.
- **CANON (2026-10-21). Character building.** The full specification is `canon/brainstorm-species.md`. In short:
  - A **phased** character takes **one affinity** (one of the 10 forms) and **two personality traits**. An **Unphased** character takes three traits and no affinity.
  - The 2024 **backgrounds are dropped**. What they gave is folded into the affinities and traits.
  - **Ability scores:** +1 from each trait and +1 from the affinity. The affinity's +1 goes to the form's primary ability: Red Strength; Green Constitution; White Wisdom; Blue Dexterity or Charisma; Black Intelligence or Charisma; Marsh Dexterity or Constitution; Soil Constitution or Strength; Oxblood Strength or Intelligence; Slate Intelligence or Wisdom; Glacier Wisdom or Charisma.
  - **Personality traits** come from one shared list, not tied to colours. Each is a personality, not a feat, with a +1 to a fixed ability and a situational benefit that any class can use. There are 12 to start, two per ability; more will be added.
  - Each form has a **signature** and **two ribbons**: Blue Trader's Tongue and amphibious swimming; Green Regeneration; Red Large; Black Guarded Mind and darkvision; White Help as a Bonus Action and flight; Marsh Swamp Walker; Soil Rooted; Oxblood Inventive Genius; Slate Search or Study as a Bonus Action; Glacier Seasoned Traveller.
  - **Darkvision belongs to Black alone.**
  - **Balance** uses a tentative point scale with a budget of about 35. It can be redone.
  - **OPEN:** several second ribbons, Hyperactive's d12 table and materials cost, and the rest of the list in that file's §10.
- **PROPOSED.** Each colour's culture has **internal disagreements** that matter more than its external rivalries. Rivalries look like **policy disputes** between neighbours who trade and intermarry, such as water rights or forests versus forges.
- **CANON (2026-10-21). The elemental wheel** (modelled on the Chinese five-phase cycle). Affinity features must fit it:
  - Feeding: Blue → Green → Red → Black → White → Blue. Water nourishes life, growth fuels fire, ash becomes ink, records preserve like ice, and ice melts to water.
  - Checking: Blue quenches Red; Red melts White; White's frost kills Green; Green's rot erases Black's records; Black's engineering dams Blue.
- **PROPOSED.** **Gold and Silver dragonborn** are nomadic **sky-readers** who predict where the sun and moon will wander. Everyone needs them, but they belong nowhere. Philosophically they oppose Black: contentment versus questioning.

## 4. Physical World

Numbers assume aspect ratio **R : r = 3 : 1** and **Earth's surface area**. Both are PROPOSED defaults; the author can change them.

| Quantity | Value |
|---|---|
| Tube radius r | ≈ 2,080 km |
| Ring radius R | ≈ 6,240 km |
| Outer equator (spine) circumference | ≈ 52,300 km |
| Inner equator (belly) circumference | ≈ 26,100 km |
| Water / land by area | 68% / 32% (generated map) |

- **PROPOSED.** **Gravity is weakest on the inner equator**, because the far side of the ring pulls "up". Even so, water and air drain toward the inner rim. The flanks slope gently toward the hole.
- **CANON (author's request for the maps).** **Mountains run along the spine; water lies on the belly.** Azhi curled **belly-in**, so the dorsal spine forms the **Spine Range** around the outer equator, and the belly cradles the ring-shaped **Inner Sea**.
- **PROPOSED.** The ring **does not spin**. That means no Coriolis effect, no trade winds, and no spinning hurricanes. A steady wind blows from the Inner Sea up the flanks toward the hot outer equator.
- **Climate (PROPOSED).** Zones follow average light. The irregular light creates weather, not chaotic zones.
  - Outer equator: hot, with a permanent rain belt. The Spine Range is drenched, with glaciers on its peaks.
  - Flanks: temperate. Inner-facing slopes are wet; the dry foothill band sits just off the spine.
  - Inner equator: dim, foggy and mild. It sees light only when Gold or Silver passes over the hole. The far side of the ring arches overhead, glowing faintly.
  - Weather is event-driven. A lingering Gold brings drought, then storms when it leaves. Long absences bring frost and fog.
  - **True Dark**: both Gold and Silver are far away. It is rare and feared, and dense cold air floods downslope into the Inner Sea basin.
- **PROPOSED. Timekeeping.** Settlements keep "watches" instead of hours and sleep when the light leaves. Trade goods include light-storing crystals and Red's eternal flames. Green's crops are bred to grow under irregular light.
- **PROPOSED. Underworld as anatomy.** Calcified arteries serve as highways. There are a petrified heart-city, lungs that exhale winds every few centuries, and a stomach of acid seas near the Bite. Bone serves as stone and ichor as oil. Magic is strongest near vital organs, and mining the god-corpse is both an industry and a moral question.
- **PROPOSED. Theme.** Azhi died of a hunger it could not satisfy. Civilizations repeating that sin echo the world's origin.

## 6. Maps and Projection

- **CANON (in use).** The world map uses the **conformal toroidal projection**, the torus equivalent of Mercator. Bearings are true, so a constant compass heading is a straight line. The map **wraps on both axes**, and printed copies tile.
  - x = θ (ringwise, headward and tailward)
  - y = (2r / √(R² − r²)) · arctan( √((R−r)/(R+r)) · tan(φ/2) ), with φ = 0 on the outer equator
  - Scale varies by row, up to (R+r)/(R−r) = 2× near the inner equator. Every map carries a **scale ladder**.
- Generated with `worldgen/`. See `worldgen/README.md`.

## 7. Art Direction

- **CANON.** **Saturday-Morning Bold** is the default for every cartoon unless the author specifies otherwise. It means thick black ink outlines, flat saturated colour and heroic proportions. Full spec: `art/STYLE.md`.
- **CANON (approved art).** Azhi is **Persian-myth-inspired, long and snakelike**, and **colossal** next to planets and the young dragons. Its design: lapis-blue body, turquoise belly plates, gold diamond ornaments and ribbon, flame-shaped tufts, curling gold barbels, curled horns and small vestigial clawed legs.
- **CANON.** In the creation scene the young world is a **sphere**, lit by Gold (warm) and Silver (cool). The young dragons carry **no visible "gift" streams** (removed 2026-10-20 as "silly").
