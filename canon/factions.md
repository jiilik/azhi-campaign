# Factions

**Status:** the concept is CANON (Troy, 2026-10-30). The rest of this file is a working draft and PROPOSED unless marked. This is the start of the factions section planned in `PLAN.md` (2c, "Factions and cultures").

## What a faction is

*(Troy, 2026-10-30)* Factions are the rules of a civilization.

- **Unaffiliated factions:** some are city or nation-state factions with no tie to any dragon.
- **Dragon factions:** others serve particular dragons, or oppose them.
- **What the faction holds:** each faction's description holds its own rules, such as standing, debts, obligations and expulsion. A form or trait only points to it.
- **Standing:** a dragonborn in good standing enjoys the faction's benefits. One who has lost its favour is an outcast and may be hunted.

### Layout of a faction entry (PROPOSED)

Each entry covers:

- who they are;
- who can join;
- what good standing gives you;
- what you owe;
- what happens if you lose favour.

## The Undertow

Indigo's secret society beneath the sea, trading in secrets, debts, dangers and forbidden things.

- **Black pearls** *(CANON, 2026-10-30)* are the tokens of debt the Undertow trades. Unless an Indigo has recently phased, they start with two. A newly phased Indigo is approached shortly with an opportunity.

### Rules moved here from the Indigo form (2026-10-30)

This was the canon text of Indigo's *Undertow* trait until 2026-10-30. It is kept word for word as the starting point for the faction's rules, and will need reworking to fit the black pearls as tokens of debt.

> Your people's hidden network reaches every port, and its currency is pearls. Once per Long Rest, while in a port or coastal settlement, you can call on the network for a small favour, such as a message passed, a door left unlocked or a name given. A favour costs a pearl. If you have none, you can take the favour on credit and owe a pearl instead; the debt can be called in at any time. Debts up to your Proficiency Bonus are ordinary business, but if you owe more, the society's collectors come calling. You can't refuse a debt that is called in: if you do, you're cast out, and you lose this trait.

### Standing (CANON, Troy, 2026-10-30)

- An Indigo in good standing with the Undertow enjoys its benefits.
- An Indigo out of its favour is an **outcast**, and possibly **hunted**.
