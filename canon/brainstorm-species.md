# Brainstorm: Species, Traits and Affinities

**Status: brainstorming only.** Nothing here is CANON or a logged decision. Everything is PROPOSED until Troy confirms it. Points that came from Troy are marked *(Troy)*; Claude's suggestions are marked *(Claude)*.

Source: voice session, 2026-10-21. Design of the Species section is still on hold (see `PLAN.md`, Phase 2a).

---

## 1. The idea: traits plus an affinity

- *(Troy)* Character creation works like a session-zero guideline. Players build their dragonborn from **personality traits** and an **elemental affinity**. The combination describes a character who has phased into that affinity and personality.
- *(Troy)* Each of the five surface colours (Blue, Green, Red, Black, White) has:
  - an **elemental affinity**, tied to physical traits (for example, Blue dragonborn swim well, Red dragonborn are strong), with mechanical bonuses;
  - a fairly **long list of personality traits** (for example, Blue: quick-witted; Red: prone to rages), each with a minor mechanical bonus.
- *(Troy)* A **phased** character picks **one affinity and two personality traits**.
- *(Claude)* This fits the myth: finding what you love is what fixes your form, so personality leading to affinity is on-theme.
- *(Claude)* Bichromes could draw their traits from either of their two adjacent colours.

## 2. Keeping it balanced

- *(Troy)* Munchkin risk is real, especially for bichromes with two colours of traits. Some interesting interactions are fine and fun, but the system must not need every combination modelled and tested.
- *(Troy)* Trait bonuses stay **minor**. Examples:
  - *Quick-witted:* a choice of +1 to Intelligence or Dexterity.
  - *Prone to rages:* no stat bonus, but a small action or temporary bonus. It must be smaller than the Barbarian's Rage.
- *(Troy, agreed with Claude)* **Don't tie traits to classes.** A class-matched bonus makes a trait the default pick for that class, and you'd need a dozen traits per colour to keep class variety.
- *(Claude, Troy agreed)* **Tie traits to situations instead.** For example, *prone to rages* gives anyone a small damage bump while Bloodied, and a Barbarian simply gets more out of it. Any class can take any trait, and the lists stay short.

## 3. Folding backgrounds in

- *(Troy)* **Drop the 2024 backgrounds** and move what they give into species design and phasing, so traits matter more than backgrounds.
- *(Claude)* This also solves the stat budget: the traits' +1s replace the background's ability score increases. The rest of a background still needs a home: an **Origin feat, two skills, a tool proficiency and starting gear**.
- *(Claude)* Knock-on effect: if your "background" lives in your phase, then phasing rewrites it. That gives the phasing penalty something real to bite on.
- Related: `PLAN.md` lists a Backgrounds item (recast for dragonborn societies) and triage deferred Character Backgrounds. Both would change if this idea is adopted.

## 5. The budget

- *(Troy)* **Point budget: 36** per character, matching the 2024 average (26 background + about 10 species).
- *(Claude, Troy agreed)* The split for a phased character:
  - each personality trait is worth about **10**;
  - the **affinity** is worth about **16**, so it is the big piece;
  - two traits + affinity = 36.
- *(Troy)* **Ability scores:** +1 from each trait and +1 from the affinity, mirroring the 2024 +1/+1/+1 option.
- *(Claude)* There is no route to the +2/+1 split, so players can't focus one stat as hard. That's a slight nerf and probably fine.

## 6. Unphased

- *(Troy)* Unphased characters are a little weaker mechanically than phased ones.
- *(Troy)* They take **three personality traits and no affinity**.
- *(Troy)* On each **Long Rest** they may **swap one trait**. They are trying on personalities as they work out who they are.
- *(Claude, Troy agreed)* Their three traits total about **30 points**, under the 36 budget, because the daily swap is itself a perk.
- *(Troy)* They get **+1 from each trait**, and the +1s **may move** when they swap. Troy expects Unphased to be an uncommon pick, chosen only by players who enjoy that daily bookkeeping.
- Open: which colours' trait lists an Unphased character may draw from (the bible's OPEN question on Unphased swaps).


## 7. Affinities (voice sessions, evening of 2026-10-21)

The target was **16 points** per colour, including the +1 to an ability score (4). The five colours now sit at **17–18.5**: a little over, which Troy accepted because he thinks skills and tools are overvalued. Points use the yardstick in §4.

### Rules that apply to every colour

- *(Troy)* **Skills are priced at 1.5 points**, not 2.
  - *(Claude, not confirmed)* That lowers each SRD background to about 25 points, so the overall budget in §5 would become about 35.
- *(Troy)* Each colour gets **two ribbons**: flavourful perks with only a very minor mechanical edge. *(Claude)* Priced at 0 points.
- *(Troy)* Features should **fit the elemental wheel**. Lightning resistance for Blue and poison resistance for Green were rejected for not fitting it.
- *(Troy)* **Avoid combat abilities you must build a class around**, such as damage abilities or melee-only reactions. If the build doesn't use them, they go to waste.
- *(Troy)* **Swapping belongs to the Unphased.** Once phased, a character is settled, so phased features aren't swappable.
- *(Troy)* **Special speeds equal walking speed:** Blue's swim, Green's climb and White's fly.
  - *(Claude)* The SRD has no species with a swim or climb speed. Its only species flight is the Dragonborn's *Draconic Flight*, with a Fly Speed equal to your Speed. A climb speed equal to walking speed matches the non-SRD Tabaxi.
- *(Troy)* **Darkvision belongs to Black alone.** It is not a shared dragonborn baseline.
- *(Troy)* Skill **choice lists** (pick two of several) worked well for Black. Return to the idea for the other colours.

### Blue: water (18 points)

*(Troy)* Blue dragonborn are marine and coastal: sailors, pirates, traders and merchants, since water moves goods easily. Their perks should give a cultural feel without being tied to a class.

| Feature | Source | Points |
|---|---|---|
| **Amphibious:** breathes air and water | Troy | 3 (Claude: Azhi is mostly water) |
| **Swim speed** equal to walking speed | Troy | 2 |
| **Slippery:** advantage on escaping grapples and restraints ("like a fish") | Claude, Troy liked it | 2 |
| **Trader's Tongue:** once per Long Rest, when you fail a Charisma check, you can reroll it | Claude, Troy added it | 3 |
| **Persuasion** proficiency (trader) | Claude, accepted | 1.5 |
| **Insight** proficiency (reading people across the bargaining table) | Troy | 1.5 |
| **Navigator's Tools** proficiency | Claude, accepted | 1 |
| **Small** size: lithe sailors, a contrast with the big Reds | Troy | 0 |
| +1 to an ability score | Troy (§5) | 4 |
| **Total** | | **18** |

- **Ribbons:**
  - *(Troy)* You always know which way the nearest water lies.
  - Second ribbon **open**. Floated: you can smell rain coming; your scales always look a little wet.
- *(Troy)* Amphibious alone is situational. One character underwater mostly scouts or fetches; a whole party underwater is a bigger undertaking. Trader's Tongue was added so Blue has a signature beyond swimming.
- *(Claude)* Being Small barely matters in the 2024 rules. The *Heavy* property depends on Strength or Dexterity of 13, not size, and Small carries the same as Medium (checked in the rules glossary). It mostly affects tight spaces and grappling bigger creatures.
- *(Claude)* There's no real SRD precedent for a social species perk. The nearest are skill grants: Human *Skillful* (any one skill) and Elf *Keen Senses* (Insight, Perception or Survival).
- Rejected:
  - lightning resistance (doesn't fit the wheel);
  - a damaging wave or riptide (unused by most classes);
  - *Flow like water*, a reaction move when an enemy comes near (only helps melee builds);
  - *Many harbours*, an extra language (overlaps Black's Comprehend Languages).

### Green: life (18 points)

*(Troy)* Green is the colour that takes a beating and keeps going. A Green barbarian or fighter is a sturdier tank, and a Green ranger becomes a non-traditional tank who burns a limited resource for a burst of staying power. Green should be strong enough that players want it. Greens end up in high places, a little cat-like, observing and scouting.

| Feature | Source | Points |
|---|---|---|
| **Regeneration** (below) | Troy (troll-inspired), shaped together | 8 (Claude: Origin-feat level, a build-around feature) |
| **Climb speed** equal to walking speed | Claude, Troy agreed | 2 |
| **Survival** proficiency (tracking: hunters, wardens, rangers in dense, overgrown country) | Troy | 1.5 |
| **Nature** proficiency | Troy | 1.5 |
| **Herbalism Kit or Poisoner's Kit**, your choice: healer or poisoner, the same plant knowledge used either way | Troy | 1 |
| +1 to an ability score | Troy (§5) | 4 |
| **Total** | | **18** |

- **Regeneration, as it stands:** as an action, spend Hit Dice up to your Proficiency Bonus (2 at level 1, 6 by level 17). Roll each and add your Constitution modifier, as on a Short Rest.
  - *(Troy)* No Bloodied requirement and no cap at half hit points: the Hit Dice are the real cost, and spending them mid-fight leaves fewer for Short Rests.
  - *(Troy)* Usable at any time, including out of combat, which lets Green skip the hour-long Short Rest for healing. Troy rejected a once-per-rest limit, to keep it strong and interesting.
  - *(Claude, not confirmed)* Taking cold damage switches it off until your next turn, so White's frost keeps its check on Green.
  - How it got here: 1 HP for a bonus action → too weak at high levels; HP equal to Proficiency Bonus → a weak trade for an action; one Hit Die → doesn't scale; Hit Dice up to Proficiency Bonus → accepted.
- **Ribbons:**
  - *(Troy)* By touching a plant, you can tell whether it's safe to eat.
  - Second ribbon still **open**. The tracking idea became the Survival feature. Not approved: plants lean toward you; flowers bloom where you sleep.
- Rejected: poison resistance (doesn't fit the wheel); moving through undergrowth without slowing.

### Red: fire and mountains (18.5 points)

*(Troy)* Red is the industrial civilization: crafters, builders and blacksmiths, with an affinity for metals. Reds are hardy and durable, suiting fighters and barbarians, and they'll often be tanks who need some mobility. They live on the **volcanic mountains**, in contrast to White's icy peaks. Troy worries players will assume Reds are big dumb brutes, so Red's culture should say "craftsman".

| Feature | Source | Points |
|---|---|---|
| **Large** from level 1, permanently, with the Goliath *Large Form* perks full-time (advantage on Strength checks) | Troy, from the Goliath | 7 |
| **Walking speed 35 feet**, as for the Goliath | Troy | 1 |
| **Two tools** of your choice | Troy | 2 |
| **Athletics or Intimidation**, your choice | Troy | 1.5 |
| **Fire resistance** | Troy | 3 (Claude: fire is the most common damage type) |
| +1 to an ability score | Troy (§5) | 4 |
| **Total** | | **18.5** |

- **Ribbons:**
  - *(Troy, from Claude's idea)* At a glance, you can judge the quality and origin of anything of value.
  - Second ribbon **open**.
- **To check:** the SRD *Large Form* also gives +10 feet of Speed. If that carries over full-time on top of a 35-foot walk, Red moves 45 feet. The voice session priced the speed as a flat 35, so the +10 probably shouldn't stack.
- *(Claude)* Being Large already doubles carrying capacity (Str × 30 lb.), so the Goliath's *Powerful Build* isn't needed for that. Its other half is advantage on ending the Grappled condition, which would overlap Blue's Slippery.
- Not chosen for the last point: crafting twice as fast; a third tool.

### Black: knowledge and ambition (18 points)

*(Troy)* Black combines the "house of knowledge" and the "house of ambition", in the style of Harry Potter. A Black character can go either way:
- **Knowledge for its own sake:** knowledge keepers, record keepers, librarians, archaeologists.
- **Knowledge as leverage:** deception and political builds, since the Black dragon wanted worshippers.

| Feature | Source | Points |
|---|---|---|
| **Guarded Mind:** advantage on Intelligence, Wisdom and Charisma saving throws (the SRD's *Gnomish Cunning*, renamed because there are no gnomes) | Claude's suggestion, Troy chose it and the name | 5 |
| **Two skills** from History, Arcana, Religion, Deception and Persuasion | Claude's list; Troy added Religion | 3 |
| **Scholar's rituals:** cast *Comprehend Languages* and *Identify* as rituals | Claude, Troy liked it | 3 |
| **Darkvision**, Black's alone | Troy | 3 |
| +1 to an ability score | Troy (§5) | 4 |
| **Total** | | **18** |

- Checked in the SRD: *Comprehend Languages* (Action or Ritual) and *Identify* (1 minute or Ritual) are both level 1 rituals. The *Ritual Caster* feat is not in the SRD, so this is our own feature.
- Darkvision range is not set. The SRD standard is 60 feet; Claude's first pass suggested a longer range.
- **Ribbons:** both **open**. Troy is still thinking.
- Rejected: a cantrip swappable on a Long Rest (swapping belongs to the Unphased). Not taken: a fixed cantrip (Message, Minor Illusion, Mage Hand); the Tiefling-style legacy spells.

### White: ice (17 points)

*(Troy)* White dragonborn are the most empathetic: healers, storytellers and keepers of oral tradition. They travel from village to village, mending broken bonds and fixing things: the diplomats and messengers. Azhi has no polar ice caps, so the only ice is on mountaintops. Whites live on the **cold mountains** and Reds on the **volcanic ones**: two mountain peoples in quiet opposition. Whites fly to rise above the chaos below, "but they land because they care."

| Feature | Source | Points |
|---|---|---|
| **Cold resistance**, mirroring Red's fire resistance | Troy | 2 (Claude: cold is less common than fire) |
| **Help as a Bonus Action** | Claude's idea, Troy loved it | 6 (advantage on demand, every turn) |
| **Flight:** once per Long Rest, from level 1, a fly speed equal to walking speed for minutes equal to your character level | Troy | 4 |
| **Medicine** proficiency | Claude, Troy agreed | 1 (Troy: mostly a roleplay skill) |
| +1 to an ability score | Troy (§5) | 4 |
| **Total** | | **17** |

- **Flight, how it got here:** the SRD's *Draconic Flight* (level 5, 10 minutes) → minutes equal to Proficiency Bonus (too much at level 1, too little at level 17) → **minutes equal to character level**: 1 minute at level 1, enough for one fight or one cliff; 20 at level 20.
- **Ribbons:**
  - *(Troy)* You're very good at memorizing short messages and keeping them, so you can serve as a courier.
  - Second ribbon **open**.
- *(Claude)* No SRD species has a feature that helps others. The non-SRD Aasimar's *Healing Hands* is the nearest. No cantrip restores hit points; *Spare the Dying* only stabilizes.
- Rejected: Healer's Kit proficiency (in the 2024 rules it is gear anyone can use; checked); the Herbalism Kit (it fits Green better). Not taken: a once-per-rest touch heal.

## 8. Bichromes (hybrids)

- *(Troy)* **Signature features belong to the primary colours.** A hybrid gets **its own new signature** that blends and shows off both parents.
- *(Claude)* Rough shape: the hybrid signature, the +1 (4), then small picks from both parents, landing around 15–16, under the primaries, as the canon power budget requires.
- *(Troy)* Hybrid skill lists **avoid stepping on a parent's core domain**. Creative builders can pick those skills up from other sources.

Wheel, clockwise: **Blue → Green → Red → Black → White → Blue**. The hybrids are the five adjacent pairs; opposed pairs can't blend.

### Blue–Green: marsh, mangrove and estuary (15.5 points)

*(Troy)* A dexterous skirmisher. The useful, unique thing here is ignoring terrain.

| +1 to an ability score | | 4 |
| **Total** | | **15.5** |

- **Swamp Walker:** you are Amphibious (as Blue), and you move through difficult terrain at full speed. Troy said "hazardous terrain"; Claude priced it as all difficult terrain.

### Green–Red: soil (16 points)

*(Troy)* Ash-fed earth, brown and umber. They are farmers, woodcutters and semi-industrialists on the fringe, plus hermits. They're burly, swinging scythes and swathing grain.

| Feature | Source | Points |
|---|---|---|
| **Rooted** (below) | Claude, Troy chose it | 8 |
| **Two skills** from Survival, Nature, Animal Handling and Perception | Troy (added Perception, dropped Athletics) | 3 |
| **One tool** from Carpenter's Tools, Woodcarver's Tools and Cook's Utensils | Claude, Troy agreed | 1 |
| +1 to an ability score | | 4 |
| **Total** | | **16** |

- **Rooted:** while standing on the ground, you have advantage on saving throws and checks against being pushed, pulled or knocked Prone, and you have **Tremorsense** out to 10 feet. The tremorsense is the strong part, because it finds invisible or hidden creatures close by.

### Red–Black: oxblood (about 15–16 points)

*(Troy)* Engineers, inventors and arms dealers. With Black's Machiavellian streak, also the **mad scientists**.

| Feature | Source | Points |
|---|---|---|
| **Inventive Genius** (below) | Claude, Troy agreed | about 7 (6–8, depending on the list) |
| **Skills** from Intimidation, Arcana and History (two picks) | Troy chose the list | 3 |
| **One tool:** Tinker's Tools or Alchemist's Supplies | Claude | 1 |
| +1 to an ability score | | 4 |
| **Total** | | **about 15** |

- **Inventive Genius:** once per Long Rest, you rig up a contraption that replicates a level 1 spell from a short, gadgety list. You choose the spell when you make it.
  - *(Troy)* **No concentration spells.** Fog Cloud is out.
  - Candidates still in: *Grease*, *Alarm*, *Feather Fall*. None needs concentration (checked).
  - *(Troy)* **Revisit the list in the spells review.** The setting may add spells of its own.
- *(Troy)* **Intimidation is the signature skill** here, between Red's physical presence and Black's ominous one. Religion (Black's core) and Athletics (Red's core) are left out.
- **Firearms check** (SRD tools file):
  - **Tinker's Tools** craft the **Musket and Pistol**.
  - **Smith's Tools** craft **Firearm Bullets**.
  - **Alchemist's Supplies** craft acid, Alchemist's Fire and oil. The SRD has **no gunpowder** item.
  - Firearms are ordinary Martial Ranged weapons, with no separate proficiency.
  - Outside the SRD, the *Gunner* feat (*Tasha's Cauldron of Everything*) covers using firearms, not making them.
  - *(Troy)* If a tool makes firearms or ammunition, it should be an option for this build. Tinker's Tools qualify.
  - *(Claude)* Smith's Tools would give the bullets but lean toward Red's lane.
  - *(Claude)* Gunpowder could become a setting item in the equipment review, perhaps from Red's volcanic sulphur or Black's ash.
