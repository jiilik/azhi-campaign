## 1. The idea: traits plus an affinity

- *(Troy)* Character creation works like a session-zero guideline. Players build their dragonborn from **personality traits** and an **elemental affinity**. The combination describes a character who has phased into that affinity and personality.
- *(Troy)* Each of the five surface colours (Blue, Green, Red, Black, White) has:
  - an **elemental affinity**, tied to physical traits (for example, Blue dragonborn swim well, Red dragonborn are strong), with mechanical bonuses;
  - a fairly **long list of personality traits** (for example, Blue: quick-witted; Red: prone to rages), each with a minor mechanical bonus.
- *(Troy)* A **phased** character picks **one affinity and two personality traits**.
- *(Claude)* This fits the myth: finding what you love is what fixes your form, so personality leading to affinity is on-theme.
- *(Claude)* Bichromes could draw their traits from either of their two adjacent colours.

## 2. Keeping it balanced

- *(Troy)* Munchkin risk is real, especially for bichromes with two colours of traits. Some interesting interactions are fine and fun, but the system must not need every combination modelled and tested.
- *(Troy)* Trait bonuses stay **minor**. Examples:
  - *Quick-witted:* a choice of +1 to Intelligence or Dexterity.
  - *Prone to rages:* no stat bonus, but a small action or temporary bonus. It must be smaller than the Barbarian's Rage.
- *(Troy, agreed with Claude)* **Don't tie traits to classes.** A class-matched bonus makes a trait the default pick for that class, and you'd need a dozen traits per colour to keep class variety.
- *(Claude, Troy agreed)* **Tie traits to situations instead.** For example, *prone to rages* gives anyone a small damage bump while Bloodied, and a Barbarian simply gets more out of it. Any class can take any trait, and the lists stay short.

## 3. Folding backgrounds in

- *(Troy)* **Drop the 2024 backgrounds** and move what they give into species design and phasing, so traits matter more than backgrounds.
- *(Claude)* This also solves the stat budget: the traits' +1s replace the background's ability score increases. The rest of a background still needs a home: an **Origin feat, two skills, a tool proficiency and starting gear**.
- *(Claude)* Knock-on effect: if your "background" lives in your phase, then phasing rewrites it. That gives the phasing penalty something real to bite on.
- Related: `PLAN.md` lists a Backgrounds item (recast for dragonborn societies) and triage deferred Character Backgrounds. Both would change if this idea is adopted.

## 5. The budget

- *(Troy)* **Point budget: 36** per character (revised to **about 35** after skills were repriced to 1.5; see §7), matching the 2024 average (26 background + about 10 species).
- *(Claude, Troy agreed)* The split for a phased character:
  - each personality trait is worth about **10**;
  - the **affinity** is worth about **16**, so it is the big piece;
  - two traits + affinity = 36.
- *(Troy)* **Ability scores:** +1 from each trait and +1 from the affinity, mirroring the 2024 +1/+1/+1 option.
- *(Claude)* There is no route to the +2/+1 split, so players can't focus one stat as hard. That's a slight nerf and probably fine.

## 6. Unphased

- *(Troy)* Unphased characters are a little weaker mechanically than phased ones.
- *(Troy)* They take **three personality traits and no affinity**.
- *(Troy)* On each **Long Rest** they may **swap one trait**. They are trying on personalities as they work out who they are.
- *(Claude, Troy agreed)* Their three traits total about **30 points**, under the 36 budget, because the daily swap is itself a perk.
- *(Troy)* They get **+1 from each trait**, and the +1s **may move** when they swap. Troy expects Unphased to be an uncommon pick, chosen only by players who enjoy that daily bookkeeping.
- Resolved: there is one shared trait list (§9), so the Unphased draw from it like everyone else.


## 7. Affinities (voice sessions, evening of 2026-10-21)

The target was **16 points** per colour, including the +1 to an ability score (4). The five colours now sit at **17–18.5**: a little over, which Troy accepted because he thinks skills and tools are overvalued. Points use the yardstick in §4.

### Rules that apply to every colour

- *(Troy)* **Skills are priced at 1.5 points**, not 2.
  - *(Troy agreed, can be redone)* That lowers each SRD background to about 25 points, so the overall budget in §5 is now **about 35**.
- *(Troy)* Each colour, **and each hybrid**, gets **two ribbons**: flavourful perks with only a very minor mechanical edge. *(Claude)* Priced at 0 points.
- *(Troy)* Features should **fit the elemental wheel**. Lightning resistance for Blue and poison resistance for Green were rejected for not fitting it.
- *(Troy)* **Avoid combat abilities you must build a class around**, such as damage abilities or melee-only reactions. If the build doesn't use them, they go to waste.
- *(Troy)* **Swapping belongs to the Unphased.** Once phased, a character is settled, so phased features aren't swappable.
- *(Troy)* **Special speeds equal walking speed:** Blue's swim, Green's climb and White's fly.
  - *(Claude)* The SRD has no species with a swim or climb speed. Its only species flight is the Dragonborn's *Draconic Flight*, with a Fly Speed equal to your Speed. A climb speed equal to walking speed matches the non-SRD Tabaxi.
- *(Troy)* **Darkvision belongs to Black alone.** It is not a shared dragonborn baseline.
- *(Troy)* Skill **choice lists** (pick two of several) worked well for Black. Return to the idea for the other colours.

### Blue: water (18 points)

*(Troy)* Blue dragonborn are marine and coastal: sailors, pirates, traders and merchants, since water moves goods easily. Their perks should give a cultural feel without being tied to a class.

| Feature | Source | Points |
|---|---|---|
| **Amphibious:** breathes air and water | Troy | 3 (Claude: Azhi is mostly water) |
| **Swim speed** equal to walking speed | Troy | 2 |
| **Slippery:** advantage on escaping grapples and restraints ("like a fish") | Claude, Troy liked it | 2 |
| **Trader's Tongue:** once per Long Rest, when you fail a Charisma check, you can reroll it | Claude, Troy added it | 3 |
| **Persuasion** proficiency (trader) | Claude, accepted | 1.5 |
| **Insight** proficiency (reading people across the bargaining table) | Troy | 1.5 |
| **Navigator's Tools** proficiency | Claude, accepted | 1 |
| **Small** size: lithe sailors, a contrast with the big Reds | Troy | 0 |
| +1 to Dexterity or Charisma | Troy (§5, §8) | 4 |
| **Total** | | **18** |

- **Ribbons:**
  - *(Troy)* You always know which way the nearest water lies.
  - Second ribbon **open**. Floated: you can smell rain coming; your scales always look a little wet.
- *(Troy)* Amphibious alone is situational. One character underwater mostly scouts or fetches; a whole party underwater is a bigger undertaking. Trader's Tongue was added so Blue has a signature beyond swimming.
- *(Claude)* Being Small barely matters in the 2024 rules. The *Heavy* property depends on Strength or Dexterity of 13, not size, and Small carries the same as Medium (checked in the rules glossary). It mostly affects tight spaces and grappling bigger creatures.
- *(Claude)* There's no real SRD precedent for a social species perk. The nearest are skill grants: Human *Skillful* (any one skill) and Elf *Keen Senses* (Insight, Perception or Survival).
- Rejected:
  - lightning resistance (doesn't fit the wheel);
  - a damaging wave or riptide (unused by most classes);
  - *Flow like water*, a reaction move when an enemy comes near (only helps melee builds);
  - *Many harbours*, an extra language (overlaps Black's Comprehend Languages).

### Green: life (18 points)

*(Troy)* Green is the colour that takes a beating and keeps going. A Green barbarian or fighter is a sturdier tank, and a Green ranger becomes a non-traditional tank who burns a limited resource for a burst of staying power. Green should be strong enough that players want it. Greens end up in high places, a little cat-like, observing and scouting.

| Feature | Source | Points |
|---|---|---|
| **Regeneration** (below) | Troy (troll-inspired), shaped together | 8 (Claude: Origin-feat level, a build-around feature) |
| **Climb speed** equal to walking speed | Claude, Troy agreed | 2 |
| **Survival** proficiency (tracking: hunters, wardens, rangers in dense, overgrown country) | Troy | 1.5 |
| **Nature** proficiency | Troy | 1.5 |
| **Herbalism Kit or Poisoner's Kit**, your choice: healer or poisoner, the same plant knowledge used either way | Troy | 1 |
| +1 to Constitution | Troy (§5, §8) | 4 |
| **Total** | | **18** |

- **Regeneration, as it stands:** as an action, spend Hit Dice up to your Proficiency Bonus (2 at level 1, 6 by level 17). Roll each and add your Constitution modifier, as on a Short Rest.
  - *(Troy)* No Bloodied requirement and no cap at half hit points: the Hit Dice are the real cost, and spending them mid-fight leaves fewer for Short Rests.
  - *(Troy)* Usable at any time, including out of combat, which lets Green skip the hour-long Short Rest for healing. Troy rejected a once-per-rest limit, to keep it strong and interesting.
  - *(Troy)* **No cold drawback.** Claude's idea of cold damage switching it off for a turn, troll-style, was scrapped.
  - How it got here: 1 HP for a bonus action → too weak at high levels; HP equal to Proficiency Bonus → a weak trade for an action; one Hit Die → doesn't scale; Hit Dice up to Proficiency Bonus → accepted.
- **Ribbons:**
  - *(Troy)* By touching a plant, you can tell whether it's safe to eat.
  - Second ribbon still **open**. The tracking idea became the Survival feature. Not approved: plants lean toward you; flowers bloom where you sleep.
- Rejected: poison resistance (doesn't fit the wheel); moving through undergrowth without slowing.

### Red: fire and mountains (18.5 points)

*(Troy)* Red is the industrial civilization: crafters, builders and blacksmiths, with an affinity for metals. Reds are hardy and durable, suiting fighters and barbarians, and they'll often be tanks who need some mobility. They live on the **volcanic mountains**, in contrast to White's icy peaks. Troy worries players will assume Reds are big dumb brutes, so Red's culture should say "craftsman".

| Feature | Source | Points |
|---|---|---|
| **Large** from level 1, permanently, with the Goliath *Large Form* perks full-time (advantage on Strength checks) | Troy, from the Goliath | 7 |
| **Walking speed 35 feet**, as for the Goliath | Troy | 1 |
| **Two tools** of your choice | Troy | 2 |
| **Athletics or Intimidation**, your choice | Troy | 1.5 |
| **Fire resistance** | Troy | 3 (Claude: fire is the most common damage type) |
| +1 to Strength | Troy (§5, §8) | 4 |
| **Total** | | **18.5** |

- **Ribbons:**
  - *(Troy, from Claude's idea)* At a glance, you can judge the quality and origin of anything of value.
  - Second ribbon **open**.
- *(Troy)* **Speed is 35 feet in total.** The SRD *Large Form*'s +10 feet does **not** stack; 45 would be too strong.
- *(Claude)* Being Large already doubles carrying capacity (Str × 30 lb.), so the Goliath's *Powerful Build* isn't needed for that. Its other half is advantage on ending the Grappled condition, which would overlap Blue's Slippery.
- Not chosen for the last point: crafting twice as fast; a third tool.

### Black: knowledge and ambition (18 points)

*(Troy)* Black combines the "house of knowledge" and the "house of ambition", in the style of Harry Potter. A Black character can go either way:
- **Knowledge for its own sake:** knowledge keepers, record keepers, librarians, archaeologists.
- **Knowledge as leverage:** deception and political builds, since the Black dragon wanted worshippers.

| Feature | Source | Points |
|---|---|---|
| **Guarded Mind:** advantage on Intelligence, Wisdom and Charisma saving throws (the SRD's *Gnomish Cunning*, renamed because there are no gnomes) | Claude's suggestion, Troy chose it and the name | 5 |
| **Two skills** from History, Arcana, Religion, Deception and Persuasion | Claude's list; Troy added Religion | 3 |
| **Scholar's rituals:** cast *Comprehend Languages* and *Identify* as rituals | Claude, Troy liked it | 3 |
| **Darkvision**, Black's alone | Troy | 3 |
| +1 to Intelligence or Charisma | Troy (§5, §8) | 4 |
| **Total** | | **18** |

- Checked in the SRD: *Comprehend Languages* (Action or Ritual) and *Identify* (1 minute or Ritual) are both level 1 rituals. The *Ritual Caster* feat is not in the SRD, so this is our own feature.
- Darkvision range is not set. The SRD standard is 60 feet; Claude's first pass suggested a longer range.
- *(Troy)* Most Black dragonborn probably live **underground**, given their darkvision.
- **Ribbons:**
  - *(Troy, adapted from Claude's "stone reader")* **Bone Reader:** you can tell whether bone, including the world's own, is natural or worked, and roughly how old it is. From its anatomy you can also tell where in Azhi's body you are and which way you're facing, which makes it underground navigation.
  - Second ribbon **open**. Not chosen: never forgetting names and ranks, dating documents, knowing what someone is proud of, sensing a held-back secret, deep sense (depth underground).
- Rejected: a cantrip swappable on a Long Rest (swapping belongs to the Unphased). Not taken: a fixed cantrip (Message, Minor Illusion, Mage Hand); the Tiefling-style legacy spells.

### White: ice (17 points)

*(Troy)* White dragonborn are the most empathetic: healers, storytellers and keepers of oral tradition. They travel from village to village, mending broken bonds and fixing things: the diplomats and messengers. Azhi has no polar ice caps, so the only ice is on mountaintops. Whites live on the **cold mountains** and Reds on the **volcanic ones**: two mountain peoples in quiet opposition. Whites fly to rise above the chaos below, "but they land because they care."

| Feature | Source | Points |
|---|---|---|
| **Cold resistance**, mirroring Red's fire resistance | Troy | 2 (Claude: cold is less common than fire) |
| **Help as a Bonus Action** | Claude's idea, Troy loved it | 6 (advantage on demand, every turn) |
| **Flight:** once per Long Rest, from level 1, a fly speed equal to walking speed for minutes equal to your character level | Troy | 4 |
| **Medicine** proficiency | Claude, Troy agreed | 1 (Troy: mostly a roleplay skill) |
| +1 to Wisdom | Troy (§5, §8) | 4 |
| **Total** | | **17** |

- **Flight, how it got here:** the SRD's *Draconic Flight* (level 5, 10 minutes) → minutes equal to Proficiency Bonus (too much at level 1, too little at level 17) → **minutes equal to character level**: 1 minute at level 1, enough for one fight or one cliff; 20 at level 20.
- **Ribbons:**
  - *(Troy)* You're very good at memorizing short messages and keeping them, so you can serve as a courier.
  - Second ribbon **open**.
- *(Claude)* No SRD species has a feature that helps others. The non-SRD Aasimar's *Healing Hands* is the nearest. No cantrip restores hit points; *Spare the Dying* only stabilizes.
- Rejected: Healer's Kit proficiency (in the 2024 rules it is gear anyone can use; checked); the Herbalism Kit (it fits Green better). Not taken: a once-per-rest touch heal.

## 8. Bichromes (hybrids)

- *(Troy)* **Signature features belong to the primary colours.** A hybrid gets **its own new signature** that blends and shows off both parents.
- *(Claude)* Rough shape: the hybrid signature, the +1 (4), then small picks from both parents, landing around 15–16, under the primaries, as the canon power budget requires.
- *(Troy)* Hybrid skill lists **avoid stepping on a parent's core domain**. Creative builders can pick those skills up from other sources.

Wheel, clockwise: **Blue → Green → Red → Black → White → Blue**. The hybrids are the five adjacent pairs; opposed pairs can't blend.

### Blue–Green: marsh, mangrove and estuary (15.5 points)

*(Troy)* A dexterous skirmisher. The useful, unique thing here is ignoring terrain.

| Feature | Source | Points |
|---|---|---|
| **Swamp Walker** (below) | Claude's idea, Troy named and built it | 8 |
| **Swim speed or climb speed**, your choice | Troy | 2 |
| **One skill** from Persuasion, Insight, Survival and Nature | Troy | 1.5 |
| +1 to Dexterity or Constitution | Troy (§8) | 4 |
| **Total** | | **15.5** |

- **Swamp Walker:** you are Amphibious (as Blue), and you move through difficult terrain at full speed. Troy said "hazardous terrain"; Claude priced it as all difficult terrain.
  - *(Troy)* It keeps the hiding: **Advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks to hide while in swamp, mud or shallow water.**
- **Ribbons:**
  - *(Troy)* **Marsh Scent:** however dirty you get, creatures that rely on smell to find you have Disadvantage on checks to detect you. A step past a pure ribbon, but narrow enough to stay at 0 points.
  - Second ribbon **open**. Not chosen: judging murky water's depth; insects don't bite; lying still in mud for an hour.

### Green–Red: soil (16 points)

*(Troy)* Ash-fed earth, brown and umber. They are farmers, woodcutters and semi-industrialists on the fringe, plus hermits. They're burly, swinging scythes and swathing grain.

| Feature | Source | Points |
|---|---|---|
| **Rooted** (below) | Claude, Troy chose it | 8 |
| **Two skills** from Survival, Nature, Animal Handling and Perception | Troy (added Perception, dropped Athletics) | 3 |
| **One tool** from Carpenter's Tools, Woodcarver's Tools and Cook's Utensils | Claude, Troy agreed | 1 |
| +1 to Constitution or Strength | Troy (§8) | 4 |
| **Total** | | **16** |

- **Rooted:** while standing on the ground, you have advantage on saving throws and checks against being pushed, pulled or knocked Prone, and you have **Tremorsense** out to 10 feet. The tremorsense is the strong part, because it finds invisible or hidden creatures close by.
- **Ribbons:**
  - *(Troy)* **Sky Sense:** you can predict the weather, and when light will come and go, over the next day. Farming is very hard without a steady day and night, so it matters on Azhi.
  - Second ribbon **open**. Not chosen: reading soil, a green thumb, the woodcutter's eye.

### Red–Black: oxblood (about 16 points)

*(Troy)* Engineers, inventors and arms dealers. With Black's Machiavellian streak, also the **mad scientists**.

| Feature | Source | Points |
|---|---|---|
| **Inventive Genius** (below) | Claude, Troy agreed | about 7 (6–8, depending on the list) |
| **Skills** from Intimidation, Arcana and History (two picks) | Troy chose the list | 3 |
| **One tool:** Tinker's Tools or Alchemist's Supplies | Claude | 1 |
| **Careful Senses** (below; counts as one of its two ribbons) | Troy | 1 |
| +1 to Strength or Intelligence | Troy (§8) | 4 |
| **Total** | | **about 16** |

- **Inventive Genius:** once per Long Rest, you rig up a contraption that replicates a level 1 spell from a short, gadgety list. You choose the spell when you make it.
  - *(Troy)* **No concentration spells.** Fog Cloud is out.
  - Candidates still in: *Grease*, *Alarm*, *Feather Fall*. None needs concentration (checked).
  - *(Troy)* **Revisit the list in the spells review.** The setting may add spells of its own.
- **Careful Senses:** if you spend 10 minutes examining an object, substance or device with your senses, you have Advantage on checks to work out what it is or how it works. It's the mad scientist's slow, careful counterpart to Red's quick judging at a glance.
  - *(Troy)* Priced at 1 point, but **counted as one of Red–Black's two ribbons**, since it is so situational.
- Second ribbon **open**. Not chosen: tinkerer's ear, steady hands, knowing a material's flash point, telling which workshop made a device.
- *(Troy)* **Intimidation is the signature skill** here, between Red's physical presence and Black's ominous one. Religion (Black's core) and Athletics (Red's core) are left out.
- **Firearms check** (SRD tools file):
  - **Tinker's Tools** craft the **Musket and Pistol**.
  - **Smith's Tools** craft **Firearm Bullets**.
  - **Alchemist's Supplies** craft acid, Alchemist's Fire and oil. The SRD has **no gunpowder** item.
  - Firearms are ordinary Martial Ranged weapons, with no separate proficiency.
  - Outside the SRD, the *Gunner* feat (*Tasha's Cauldron of Everything*) covers using firearms, not making them.
  - *(Troy)* If a tool makes firearms or ammunition, it should be an option for this build. Tinker's Tools qualify.
  - *(Claude)* Smith's Tools would give the bullets but lean toward Red's lane.
  - *(Claude)* Gunpowder could become a setting item in the equipment review, perhaps from Red's volcanic sulphur or Black's ash.

### Black–White: slate (about 15 points)

*(Troy)* Black's aptitude, tempered by White: knowledge and power used for good, not as ends in themselves. Slate are the **investigators** and detectives, with a strong dash of **Gandalf the Grey**, the wandering sage who notices what others miss. They lean into **knowledge-type intuition** (clues, places, lore), as distinct from glacier's character intuition. Some players may play against type, turning the investigator's skill set into a **cat burglar**.

| Feature | Source | Points |
|---|---|---|
| **Keen Investigator** (below; name is a placeholder) | Claude, shaped by Troy | about 7 |
| **Two skills** from Investigation, Perception, History and Religion | Troy (added History and Religion, dropped Medicine) | 3 |
| **One tool:** Calligrapher's Supplies, Thieves' Tools or the Forgery Kit | Claude's list, Troy chose a three-way choice | 1 |
| +1 to Intelligence or Wisdom | Troy (§8) | 4 |
| **Total** | | **about 15** |

- **Keen Investigator:**
  - You can take the **Search or Study action as a Bonus Action**. *(Troy added Study.)* In the SRD, Search covers Insight, Medicine, Perception and Survival, and Study covers Arcana, History, Investigation, Nature and Religion (checked). *(Troy)* It's very useful for seeing through illusions in combat without losing your action.
  - *(Troy)* Insight **stays in**: a slate who gets Insight from another source can use it this way.
  - You can cast **Detect Magic**, **Augury** and **Find Traps** as rituals. *(Troy)* Find Traps isn't normally a ritual (SRD: level 2, Action); it's a ritual for this build only. Note that *Detect Magic* needs Concentration, and *Augury*'s omen depends on the DM.
- *(Claude)* Religion breaks the rule of leaving out a parent's core skill (it was kept off Red–Black's list as Black's). Troy kept it here.
- Calligrapher's Supplies, as checked in the SRD: their Utilize action writes text that *guards against* forgery. The Forgery Kit mimics handwriting and copies seals.
- Rejected:
  - *Tutor*, teaching an ally a skill (Troy went the investigator way);
  - a once-per-Long-Rest question to the DM (Troy: too subject to the DM's whims);
  - *Spot the Weakness*;
  - *Detect Poison and Disease* (not the right theme);
  - *Identify* (already Black's);
  - Cartographer's Tools (Blue's side).
- *(Claude)* No SRD species has anything like this. The nearest is the non-SRD *Observant* feat (2024 version: +1 to Intelligence or Wisdom, a skill or Expertise, Search as a Bonus Action). It isn't in the SRD, so the details are from Claude's knowledge.
- **Ribbons:**
  - *(Troy)* **Uncanny Timekeeping:** you always know the exact time, even underground or with no sky to read. That's rare on a world with no steady day and night.
  - Second ribbon **open**. Not chosen: never forgetting faces, reading handwriting, dating stains, smoke shapes, advice that sticks.

### White–Blue: glacier (about 13 points)

*(Troy)* White's couriers and Blue's merchants combine into the **ultimate traveller**: envoys, guides, wandering diplomats and caravan masters. Their knowledge is **lived wisdom**, not book learning, and they lean into **character intuition**. They show up in town, find the inn and get the best room. They know domesticated animals; the Green–Red farmers know the wild ones.

| Feature | Source | Points |
|---|---|---|
| **Seasoned Traveller** (below) | Claude, shaped by Troy | about 5 |
| **Two skills** from Insight, Medicine, Survival and Animal Handling | Claude's list; Troy dropped, then restored, Animal Handling | 3 |
| **One tool:** Cartographer's Tools or Cook's Utensils | Claude, Troy agreed | 1 |
| +1 to Wisdom or Charisma | Troy (§8) | 4 |
| **Total** | | **about 13** |

- **Seasoned Traveller:**
  - *(Troy)* You can **control any willing Beast at least one size larger than you** as a mount, even if it isn't trained to accept a rider. *(Claude)* The SRD already makes any willing creature one size larger a possible mount, but it can only be *controlled* if trained. This removes the training requirement. Troy picked this over limiting it to domesticated animals, accepting that it edges toward Green–Red's lane.
  - *(Troy)* **Advantage on checks to drive or steer a vehicle, and to keep a mount under control.** The 2024 SRD has no vehicle proficiency (checked), so this defines the benefit.
  - *(Troy)* **Heard It on the Road:** in a settlement, you know the local customs, who's in charge, and a rumour or two.
  - Not chosen: *Tireless Road* (Fast pace with no drawback); *Never Lost*.
- Persuasion is left off the skill list as Blue's core.
- Rejected:
  - *Read the Room* (Troy: too complicated mechanically);
  - a choice of swim or fly speed (flight stays White's signature; Blue–Green already has a speed choice);
  - walking easily on ice and snow (not taken).
- **Ribbons:**
  - *(Troy)* **A gaming set of your choice:** the card shark at every inn. Normally a tool proficiency, but kept as a ribbon.
  - Second ribbon **open**.

### Which ability the +1 goes to

*(Troy)* Each form's +1 goes to a **primary ability** linked to that colour, so the bonus has a preferred home. There are six abilities and five colours, so Charisma is shared by two colours. Claude's suggestions, with Troy adding Charisma to Blue:

| Colour | +1 to | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Red | Strength | The big craftsmen |
| Green | Constitution | Takes a beating and keeps going |
| White | Wisdom | The healers and empathetic ones |
| Blue | Dexterity **or** Charisma | Lithe sailors who slip free of grapples; traders who talk for a living (Trader's Tongue) |
| Black | Intelligence **or** Charisma | The scholar and the schemer |

*(Troy)* **Hybrids choose between their parents' abilities, trimmed to two.** Four of the five would otherwise have had three options; only soil already had two.

| Hybrid | +1 to | Dropped |
|---|---|---|
| Marsh (Blue–Green) | Dexterity **or** Constitution | Charisma: it's a dexterous skirmisher |
| Soil (Green–Red) | Constitution **or** Strength | (only two to begin with) |
| Oxblood (Red–Black) | Strength **or** Intelligence | Charisma. *(Claude)* Its signature skill, Intimidation, uses Charisma; Troy dropped it anyway. |
| Slate (Black–White) | Intelligence **or** Wisdom | Charisma: clues plus insight |
| Glacier (White–Blue) | Wisdom **or** Charisma | Dexterity: lived wisdom and diplomacy |

- This covers the affinity's +1. Each personality trait carries its own fixed +1 (§9). The Unphased have no affinity, so this doesn't apply to them.
- Gold and Silver are left out for now, since whether they can be reached is still open.

## 9. Personality traits (voice session, late evening of 2026-10-21)

### How traits work

- *(Troy)* **One shared list.** Traits are **not tied to colours**; anyone can take any trait. Synergies with other traits and with affinities will come out naturally. *(Claude)* This settles the old question of which lists the Unphased draw from: there is only the one.
- *(Troy)* Start with **12 traits, two per ability**, so the +1s are spread evenly. Grow to about 20 later.
- *(Troy)* A trait is a **personality**, not a feat. The name is who you are ("prone to rages"), and the benefit flows from it, worded as clearly as a feat.
- *(Claude)* Budget: about 10 points per trait. The +1 is 4, leaving **about 6** for the benefit, roughly the size of Savage Attacker or Skilled.
- *(Troy)* **Flavour first.** Don't chase exact points at the expense of flavour, but every trait needs a **real mechanical benefit**.
- *(Claude, not confirmed)* Light Sleeper and Heavy Sleeper can't be taken together.

### The twelve

| Ability | Trait | Trait |
|---|---|---|
| Strength | Prone to Rages | Reckless |
| Dexterity | Light Sleeper | Hyperactive |
| Constitution | Heavy Sleeper | Iron Stomach |
| Intelligence | The Collector | Quizmaster |
| Wisdom | The Journalist | The Skeptic |
| Charisma | Earworm | The Artist |

#### Strength

**Prone to Rages** *(Troy's idea; Claude's wording, Troy approved)*
- +1 to Strength.
- While you're Bloodied, you have Advantage on Strength saving throws.
- Once per turn while you're Bloodied, when you hit with a melee attack, you add your Proficiency Bonus to the damage.

**Reckless** *(Claude, Troy chose it)*
- +1 to Strength.
- You have Advantage on Initiative rolls.
- The first attack roll against you in each combat has Advantage.
- *(Troy)* Advantage on Initiative is significant. *(Claude)* About 5 points with the drawback, close enough to 6.

Not chosen for Strength:
- Show-Off (Advantage on Strength checks when watched).
- *Hard Labourer:* carry as one size larger, plus Advantage on breaking things. Troy noted that with Red it would mean Huge-sized carrying, and Red already has Advantage on Strength checks.
- *Brawler:* too feat-like, close to the non-SRD *Tavern Brawler*.
- Stubborn; Competitive.
- *Bodyguard:* as a Reaction, pull an adjacent ally 5 feet aside. Troy wants it as a **Battle Master maneuver** in the class work.

#### Dexterity

**Light Sleeper** *(Troy's idea: "someone who had too much coffee")*
- +1 to Dexterity.
- While you're resting or asleep, you can't be Surprised, and any noise louder than a whisper wakes you.
- You finish a Long Rest after 4 hours of sleep (like the Elf's *Trance*).
- Checked in the SRD: there's no surprise round in the 2024 rules. Being Surprised gives Disadvantage on your Initiative roll.
- *(Troy)* It stays a Dexterity trait, even though alertness could read as Wisdom.

**Hyperactive** *(Troy's idea)*
- +1 to Dexterity.
- **Idle hands:** once per day (Long Rest), you make a small object and roll a d12 to see what it is:

| d12 | Result |
|---|---|
| 1–4 | A worthless trinket |
| 5–8 | Something worth a few silver |
| 9–10 | A small useful item, such as a fishing lure, a whistle or a lockpick |
| 11 | Something worth a few gold |
| 12 | Something special (DM's choice) |

- **Busy with your tools:** once per Long Rest, instead of rolling, you can make one item from the Craft list of a tool you're proficient with, worth up to 50 GP. *(Troy)* A hyperactive character uses their tools at every spare moment, and this should be worth something to them.
  - Checked in the SRD: the Herbalism Kit crafts the *Potion of Healing* (50 GP), as well as Antitoxin, Candles and the Healer's Kit. That ties in with Green's Herbalism Kit and Red's tools.
- **Open:** fill in the actual d12 items; decide whether the tool crafting costs materials. The SRD's crafting rules normally charge half the item's price.

Not chosen for Dexterity:
- Jumpy (overlaps Light Sleeper).
- Light-Fingered; Cautious.
- *Skittish* and *Flinches*, two reaction ideas. Troy had wanted something for reactions, then went with Hyperactive.

#### Constitution

**Heavy Sleeper** *(Troy's idea, a counterpoint to Light Sleeper)*
- +1 to Constitution.
- If you're woken from sleep, you have Disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks for your first two rounds.
- When you finish a Long Rest with uninterrupted sleep, you gain Temporary Hit Points equal to your character level.
- *(Claude, Troy agreed)* Temporary hit points equal to your character level instead of Troy's first idea, Proficiency Bonus, so it scales.

**Iron Stomach** *(Claude's "Glutton", renamed by Troy)*
- +1 to Constitution.
- You have Advantage on saving throws against poison or disease that you've eaten or drunk. *(Troy: it's the stomach that's tough, not the blood.)*
- **Abs of steel:** +1 to AC while you aren't wearing armour. *(Troy's joke idea; Claude made it conditional to fit the budget.)*
- Dropped: *Forager's Gut* (raw, spoiled or strange food counts as a ration), which made way for the AC.
- Not added: resistance to getting drunk. Checked: the SRD has no drinking or intoxication rules (Brewer's Supplies can only identify alcohol), so Troy left it out.
- Not chosen: Stoic; Restless Wanderer; *Hardy Appetite*; extra healing from a meal on a Short Rest (Troy: the theme is surviving what you eat, not eating).

#### Intelligence

**The Collector** *(Troy's idea: someone who indexes everything)*
- +1 to Intelligence.
- You have Advantage on checks to find something in a library, archive or collection.
- You always know what's in your pack, and pulling out any item doesn't take an action.
- *(Troy)* You notice at once when a rare object is within sight, even hidden among clutter. *(Claude)* This works alongside Red's ribbon: Red judges value, the Collector spots rarity.
- Dropped: finding things in a tenth of the usual time (Troy).

**Quizmaster** *(Troy's idea)*
- +1 to Intelligence.
- Proficiency in one Intelligence skill of your choice: Arcana, History, Investigation, Nature or Religion.
- **Trivia:** on Intelligence checks to recall lore, you add half your Proficiency Bonus if you aren't already proficient.
- Rejected: *Pop Quiz*; *Tip of the Tongue* (turn a failed recall into a success).

#### Wisdom

**The Journalist** *(Troy's idea: someone who keeps a diary, not a reporter)*
- +1 to Wisdom.
- If you write in your journal before a Long Rest, you gain Heroic Inspiration when it ends. This is the Human's *Resourceful* (checked), with reflection as the price.
- You can look up any detail you've written down, such as a name, a face or a route, and get it right.
- *(Troy)* It lets players bring their own note-taking into the character: the player's notes count as the character's knowledge.

**The Skeptic** *(Troy's name; Claude's draft, not discussed in detail)*
- +1 to Wisdom.
- Proficiency in Insight.
- You have Advantage on checks and saving throws to see through illusions, and on Insight checks to tell when someone is lying.
- *(Claude)* It counters Earworm, whose Disadvantage on Insight it would cancel.

#### Charisma

**Earworm** *(Troy's idea)*
- +1 to Charisma.
- Proficiency in Performance. *(Troy)* No affinity offers Performance, so it needs a home.
- Once per Long Rest, after a performance, up to six creatures who heard it have Disadvantage on Insight checks against you until your next Long Rest.
- *(Troy)* Seed the tune in the room, then work the room later, while the whole town hums it.
- *(Claude)* "Until your next Long Rest" replaces Troy's "until the end of the day", since Azhi has no steady day.
- Replaced: an earlier draft that gave you Advantage on other Charisma checks against them for an hour.

**The Artist** *(Troy's idea; "Artist" rather than "Artisan", since Red is the craftsman)*
- +1 to Charisma.
- Proficiency with Painter's Supplies, Calligrapher's Supplies or a musical instrument.
- If you give someone a piece of your art, or play for them, you have Advantage on your next Charisma check with them. *(Troy added the performance part.)*
- **Muse:** once per Long Rest, another creature who sees or hears your art gains Heroic Inspiration. *(Troy)* The artist really helps others, and the recipient chooses when to use it.
- Rejected: an ally regaining a Hit Die (Troy: no); painting-only ideas (Troy: must work for any art form); *Patronage* (free lodging and meals, too light).
- Not chosen: *Signature Style*; *Artist's Eye*; *Uplifting Work*.
