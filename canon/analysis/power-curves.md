## Findings (Claude, 2026-11-12, for Troy)

1. **The phasing penalty is large at this rate.** Phasing every five levels, a species-first character holds **8 of 12** points at level 12, an even split **9**; grid-only keeps all 12. Each phase halves whatever is on the species wheel at that moment, so investing there right before a phase is the worst case.
2. **Hit points drop on phasing**, because lost rings take their Hit Dice: a species-first Herald goes from 18 HP at level 4 to 14 at level 5. That's unusual in D&D, where hit points don't go down on level-up.
3. **Bonuses fall behind.** By level 12 grid-only builds have 2 ability +1s, about 2 Prof. Ups and 2 Attack Ups; species-first builds have 1 of each.
4. **Parity holds only without phasing.** With no phase, six deep on either wheel is about even, as designed. The halving makes the species wheel a worse investment the more often a character phases.
5. **Archetypes look alike in shape**; the differences are Hit Die size. The Mage line is the thinnest (39 HP at 12 grid-only, against a 2024 wizard's 50 without Con).
