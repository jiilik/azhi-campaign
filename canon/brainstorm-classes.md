# Brainstorm: Classes and Subclasses on Azhi

**Status: ON HOLD** (Troy, 2026-11-05: players start with the PHB's default classes, reflavoured as needed; nothing gets fixed yet). PROPOSED, opened 2026-11-04. An evaluation of the twelve SRD 5.2.1 classes, their SRD subclasses, and the other subclasses in the 2024 *Player's Handbook*, against the setting. Nothing here is canon until Troy approves it. Everything is *(Claude)* unless marked *(Troy)*.

**Sources.**
- SRD classes: `srd/03-classes/`, checked line by line for planes, creature types, deities, languages and other species.
- Non-SRD subclasses: the 48 names in the 2024 *Player's Handbook* are verified against WotC's published list (ComicBook.com, 2024). Their **mechanics are not in the SRD** and are described here only by concept, from memory. Check them against the book before any player guide goes out. None of their text is used.
- Builds on `canon/audit-planes.md` (2026-10-31), which this file supersedes for classes.

**Verdict in one line:** all twelve classes fit. The trouble is concentrated in the **divine trio** (cleric, paladin, warlock), which assume gods, Outer Planes, fiends and celestials. The rest need only reflavouring or a word changed.

## 1. Cross-cutting issues

These touch several classes. Settling them first makes the class-by-class pass quick.

| # | Issue | Where it bites | Proposed fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| X1 | **"Divine energy directly from the Outer Planes."** The Outer Planes are scrapped (2026-11-01). | Cleric and paladin Channel Divinity | Divine energy comes **from the dragons**. Fits "the first clerics channelled the dragons" (Troy, 2026-11-04). |
| X2 | **"Your deity or pantheon."** | Cleric Divine Intervention; Life Domain and Oath of Devotion flavour text | "Your dragon, or the Seven." The gods are real, alive and within flying distance, which is a gift for play. |
| X3 | **Scrapped creature types:** Celestial, Fiend, Fey. | Paladin Divine Sense and Holy Ward; druid Wild Companion (the familiar is Fey); warlock Pact of the Chain forms (Imp, Quasit, Sprite); Fiend Patron throughout | Swap in what Azhi has: Aberrations (things from beyond), Constructs (the machines), Undead (if kept). Wait on monster change B1 (*Find Familiar*) and spell B2 (*Find Steed*). |
| X4 | **Undead are OPEN.** | Cleric Turn Undead; paladin Divine Sense and Holy Ward; Life Domain | If undead stay, this is lore gold: **clerics emerged in epoch 3 to counter necromancy** (Troy), so Turn Undead is the order's founding purpose. If they go, Turn Undead needs a new target (Aberrations?). |
| X5 | ***Wish*:** only Indigo keeps it today, and it's answered by a vestige of Azhi (PROPOSED lore). | Wizard and sorcerer 9th-level lists; cleric **Greater Divine Intervention** (level 20 can choose *Wish*) | Wizards and sorcerers learn it only from Indigo or by rediscovery. The cleric's level-20 feature is the sharp one: does a dragon grant *Wish* when the dragons themselves can't cast above level 9? Options: keep it (the dragon brokers a request to Azhi's vestige); swap it for a different capstone. |
| X6 | **"Draconic" is everyone.** Every character is a dragonborn. | Sorcerer Draconic Sorcery; *Dragon's Breath* (on sorcerer and wizard lists) vs "no breath weapons" (2026-10-30); Dragon Companion summons a dragon, and dragons are gods | Reframe Draconic Sorcery as **a personal gift from one of the Seven** (see §3, Sorcerer). *Dragon's Breath* goes to the spell-changes list. |
| X7 | **Class languages:** Druidic and Thieves' Cant. Languages are regional (2026-10-30). | Druid level 1; rogue level 1 | Keep both: they're secret jargons, not regional languages, so the rule isn't broken. Thieves' Cant could be **the Undertow's cant**. |
| X8 | **Religion in an age that rejects the gods.** The present may be an "enlightenment" (PROPOSED, epoch notes). | Cleric, paladin, Zealot barbarian | Clerics become a counterculture or an establishment under pressure. Either is a good story; it's Troy's call. |
| X9 | **Phasing and faith.** | Cleric, paladin, Draconic sorcerer, warlock | If a Black cleric phases to Green, do they keep their god? Proposed: **yes, faith is a choice, not a colour**, matching "affinity is not inherited". A Green who still serves Black is a strong character. |

## 2. When each tradition began

Ties the classes into the epochs and spell origins. All PROPOSED.

| Tradition | Origin |
|---|---|
| Wizard | Epoch 2: the Seven teach magic during the machine war. Wizards are the dragons' students' students. |
| Sorcerer | Magic in the blood: a gift of a dragon (Draconic), or the vestige of Azhi seeping from the corpse (Wild Magic). Magic is strongest near the vital organs (PROPOSED). |
| Cleric, paladin | Epoch 3: channelling the dragons, first to counter necromancy (Troy). |
| Druid | Primal magic is Green's teaching; Wild Shape is shape-changing, so it dates from epoch 3 (spell origins). Druids before epoch 3 had no Wild Shape. |
| Warlock | Any epoch: a bargain with a power that isn't your god. |
| Monk | White's mountain monasteries (CANON society). |
| Martial classes | Always. |

## 3. Class by class

Each class lists the **SRD subclass** first, then the **other 2024 PHB subclasses** (concept only). Fit ratings:

- **Fits:** works as written.
- **Reflavour:** works with new wording; no mechanical change.
- **Rework:** needs mechanical change.
- **Drop:** doesn't fit the setting.

### Barbarian
Class: **fits.** Rage is a primal power. Suggested home: Red's mines and forges, or anywhere.
- **Berserker** (SRD): fits.
- Wild Heart (animal spirits): fits; real-world animals are canon.
- World Tree (a cosmic tree linking the planes): **reflavour** as **Azhi's spine**, the infinitely stiff thing holding the world together. Very on-theme.
- Zealot (divine fury): **reflavour** for Black's zealots (CANON society).

### Bard
Class: **fits.** Suggested home: Blue's port cities (the cosmopolitan crossroads), the keepers of variant tellings of the myth.
- **Lore** (SRD): fits. Grey uncovers, Black hoards, Yellow gives away: lore bards sit between them.
- Dance, Valor: fit.
- Glamour (Feywild-flavoured): **reflavour** away from the Fey; a natural Indigo or *Masquerade* partner.

### Cleric
Class: **reflavour** (X1, X2, X4, X5). The domains map onto the Seven well.
- **Life** (SRD): Green (most healing, per spell origins). Its text names "agricultural deities": Green or Brown.
- Light: Gold (light and radiant magic). The catch: Gold takes no interest in those below (CANON). A Light cleric channels a god who never answers.
- Trickery: Black (illusion, stealth) or Indigo.
- War: Red, or White (defence, battlefield control).
- **Gaps:** no SRD or PHB domain for Blue (water, trade) or Silver (travel, detection). Candidates for **new domains** later.

### Druid
Class: **reflavour.** Wild Companion's familiar is Fey (X3). Druidic is fine (X7).
- **Land** (SRD): fits, and the land is a corpse: arid, polar, temperate and tropical become regions of Azhi's body.
- Moon: Silver.
- Sea: Blue.
- Stars: the sky-readers (PROPOSED Gold and Silver culture) and the Astral, which is kept.

### Fighter
Class: **fits.** Suggested home: Red smiths, White's defenders, anyone.
- **Champion** (SRD), Battle Master, Eldritch Knight: fit.
- Psi Warrior: **reflavour.** Azhi has no psionics. Candidates: Black's Guarded Mind tradition, or a discipline learned from the thinking machines.
- *(Troy's backlog: a Warlord class. Battle Master covers some of that ground.)*

### Monk
Class: **fits.** White's mountain monasteries are canon, so the monk has a home before anything else does.
- **Open Hand** (SRD): fits.
- Elements: fits, and better than in most settings: the myth's "fire, water, earth and ice", and the four Elemental Planes are kept.
- Mercy: fits; Green-leaning.
- Shadow: **reflavour** away from the Shadowfell; Indigo's or Black's.

### Paladin
Class: **reflavour, light rework** (X1, X3, X4). Divine Sense detects Celestials, Fiends and Undead: two of those are gone.
- **Devotion** (SRD): its text mentions gods of law and angels. Reflavour to devotion to a dragon, or to an ideal.
- Glory, Vengeance: fit.
- Ancients (fey-tinged nature): **reflavour** to Green.
- **Idea:** oaths don't need gods. In an age rejecting the gods, a paladin's oath may be sworn **against** them. "We will no longer play your games."

### Ranger
Class: **fits.** The sprig of mistletoe is fine (real flora).
- **Hunter** (SRD): fits.
- Beast Master: fits (real animals).
- Gloom Stalker (the Underdark): **reflavour** for **the Underworld as anatomy**: arteries, the heart-city. A natural fit for Black, with its 120-foot Darkvision.
- Fey Wanderer: **drop or rework.** It's Feywild through and through. Could return with the Fey "incursions from beyond", if those happen.

### Rogue
Class: **fits.** Suggested home: Indigo and the Undertow.
- **Thief** (SRD), Arcane Trickster: fit.
- Assassin: fits; Indigo (CANON spies and assassins).
- Soulknife (psionic): **reflavour**, as for the Psi Warrior.

### Sorcerer
Class: **fits.** The interesting class here, since everyone has draconic blood.
- **Draconic Sorcery** (SRD): **reflavour** as a **gift from one of the Seven** (X6). Elemental Affinity's five types (acid, cold, fire, lightning, poison) are D&D's chromatic set and don't quite match Azhi's dragons. Options: keep the five; or map them to the Seven (Red fire, White cold, Gold lightning, Black acid or force, Green poison, Blue and Silver open). Dragon Wings at level 14 overlaps White's flight; probably fine at that level. Dragon Companion summons a dragon, which on Azhi means a god's avatar or a lesser drake.
- Wild Magic: **reflavour** as **Azhi's vestige** leaking through, or the thinning of reality (2026-11-01). Excellent fit.
- Clockwork (Mechanus): **reflavour** as **the thinking machines' legacy**, as `audit-planes.md` suggested. A sorcerer touched by *The Last Machine*.
- Aberrant (the Far Realm): **reflavour** as **things from beyond**, the thinning-boundaries horror. Fits well.

### Warlock
Class: **rework.** Its only SRD subclass, the **Fiend Patron**, is built on the Lower Planes, and level 14's Hurl Through Hell sends a target through them. Pact of the Chain's Imp, Quasit and Sprite are gone (X3). *Contact Other Plane* at level 9 depends on which planes remain.
- **The good news:** Azhi is full of patrons that aren't gods. Candidates:
  - **The young Black**, recruiting for its bid at godhood. Your power comes with an agenda.
  - **Things from beyond**, reaching through the thinning walls.
  - **A surviving machine**, such as the thinking engine under its glassy crater.
  - **The vestige of Azhi** itself: the hunger that answers *Wish*. A "finish the meal" warlock.
  - **The Undertow**: power on credit, paid in black pearls. Faction debt as a pact.
- **Fiend Patron** (SRD): **rework.** Keep its mechanics (temporary HP, Dark One's luck, resilience) and rename and reflavour it for one of those patrons. Hurl Through Hell needs a new destination: perhaps the Elemental Planes, or a moment inside Azhi's memory.
- Great Old One: **reflavour** as things from beyond. Probably the best fit of the four.
- Archfey, Celestial: **drop or rework** (Feywild and Upper Planes scrapped). Celestial's healing-and-radiance kit could be rebuilt as a **Gold or Silver patron**: the distant god who, unusually, did take an interest.

### Wizard
Class: **fits.** The cleanest class in the setting: the dragons taught magic, and wizards study it.
- **Evoker** (SRD): fits; Black (force and destruction).
- Abjurer: White (protection).
- Diviner: Silver (detection) or Grey (uncovers).
- Illusionist: Black or Indigo.
- *Wish* (X5).

## 5. Decisions for Troy

Numbered so they can be approved or rejected one by one.

1. **Divine source:** cleric and paladin Channel Divinity draws on **the dragons**, replacing "the Outer Planes" (X1).
2. **Deity wording:** "deity or pantheon" becomes "your dragon, or the Seven" (X2).
3. **Faith survives phasing** (X9).
4. **Cleric level 20 and *Wish*:** keep it (the dragon brokers a request to Azhi's vestige), or replace the capstone (X5).
5. **Warlock patrons:** adopt some or all of the five candidates (young Black, things from beyond, a surviving machine, Azhi's vestige, the Undertow).
6. **Fiend Patron:** keep its mechanics, rename and reflavour it for one patron; Hurl Through Hell gets a new destination.
7. **Druid Wild Companion** and **Pact of the Chain forms:** decide alongside monster change B1 (*Find Familiar*).
8. **Paladin Divine Sense and Holy Ward:** decide after the undead question; meanwhile, target Aberrations in place of Celestials and Fiends.
9. **Draconic Sorcery** reframed as a gift from one of the Seven; Elemental Affinity types kept as the SRD's five, or remapped.
10. **Thieves' Cant** becomes the Undertow's cant (optional flavour).
11. **Non-SRD subclasses:** produce a player guide rating them (fits / reflavour / rework / drop), as in §4. It names them only, with no rules text.
12. **New subclasses to design** later: a Blue cleric domain (water, trade), a Silver cleric domain (travel, detection), Azhi-specific warlock patrons.
13. **Class homes** (§3): note a suggested society for each class in the player guide (monk–White, rogue–Indigo, and so on), as suggestions, not limits.
