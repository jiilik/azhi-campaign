# Minigame: Famous Characters, Two Builds

A quick, informal way to test whether the character-building system covers familiar archetypes (Troy, 2026-10-25).

## Rules

1. Troy names a famous character from fiction.
2. Claude proposes **two builds** that fit the character's archetype reasonably well. Each build is a form (one of the nine colours, or the Unphased) plus personality traits, with a line on why it fits.
3. The two builds for a character **share no personality traits and no colour**. Adjacent colours on the wheel and similar traits are fine.
4. A game runs **five rounds** unless Troy says otherwise.
5. At the end, Claude may note which traits and forms went unused, as a hint at which ones need stronger hooks.

## Recording

**Outputs go in the chat only and are never recorded.** The builds are speculative and stay out of the repo, the decision log, the Claude Docs and memory. Only these rules are kept here.
