# Brainstorm: The Deep Timeline (Epochs)

**Status: PROPOSED** (opened 2026-10-31). This is the working record for Azhi's deep history. Troy's statements are marked *(Troy)*; nothing here is canon until he approves it.

## 1. Troy's dump (2026-10-31)

- **The myth stands.** *(Troy)* `azhi-myth.md` and the `.rtf` are still canonical. But much may have happened between the legend and the present day of play: a **significant amount of time**, even **geological** amounts, given the erosion and landscape changes on the map since Azhi's death.
- **Eras with epic shake-ups** *(Troy)*: like the major eras in Final Fantasy XIV, each ending in a shake-up of the whole civilization.
  - Build a **deep timeline** of these shake-ups, with **geography** to match: ruins of ancient cities, former dragon lairs and so on.
- **Six epochs before the present** *(Troy)*, each ending in some catastrophe:
  1. The first is **Azhi eating the original planet**.
  2. The second starts at the **formation of this world**.
  3. *Etc.:* details to be filled in later.
- **No more hybrids or colour mixing** *(Troy, planned)*: remove every reference to hybrids, blends and colour mixing, and write **new lore explaining how the other colours emerged**.
- **Yellow and Grey** *(Troy)*:
  - In one epoch, the **Black dragon** tried to create new adult dragons, **Yellow and Grey**, under its direct control: **homunculi** of the true Gold and Silver dragons, who largely don't care.
  - It was an attempt to **subvert the few Gold and Silver dragonborn** on the world by forcing them under a new hierarchy.
  - **It worked:** every Gold and Silver dragonborn of that era was converted to Yellow or Grey.
  - The **other dragons aligned to kill the Yellow and Grey homunculi** in an epic conclusion, in the style of *The Lord of the Rings*.
  - **Afterwards,** Yellow and Grey dragonborn continue to be born, but they **align with the Gold and Silver dragons**.
  - There are **rumours of true Gold and Silver dragonborn** in later eras, but the information is patchy.
  - **To do:** choose which epoch this happened in. Perhaps the one just before the present.
