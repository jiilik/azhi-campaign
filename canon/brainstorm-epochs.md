# Brainstorm: The Deep Timeline (Epochs)

**Status: PROPOSED** (opened 2026-10-31). This is the working record for Azhi's deep history. Troy's statements are marked *(Troy)*; nothing here is canon until he approves it.

## 1. Troy's dump (2026-10-31)

- **The myth stands.** *(Troy)* `azhi-myth.md` and the `.rtf` are still canonical. But much may have happened between the legend and the present day of play: a **significant amount of time**, even **geological** amounts, given the erosion and landscape changes on the map since Azhi's death.
- **Eras with epic shake-ups** *(Troy)*: like the major eras in Final Fantasy XIV, each ending in a shake-up of the whole civilization.
  - Build a **deep timeline** of these shake-ups, with **geography** to match: ruins of ancient cities, former dragon lairs and so on.
- **Six epochs before the present** *(Troy)*, each ending in some catastrophe:
  1. The first is **Azhi eating the original planet**.
  2. The second starts at the **formation of this world**.
  3. *Etc.:* details to be filled in later.
- **No more hybrids or colour mixing** *(Troy, planned)*: remove every reference to hybrids, blends and colour mixing, and write **new lore explaining how the other colours emerged**.
- **Yellow and Grey** *(Troy)*:
  - In one epoch, the **Black dragon** tried to create new adult dragons, **Yellow and Grey**, under its direct control: **homunculi** of the true Gold and Silver dragons, who largely don't care.
  - It was an attempt to **subvert the few Gold and Silver dragonborn** on the world by forcing them under a new hierarchy.
  - **It worked:** every Gold and Silver dragonborn of that era was converted to Yellow or Grey.
  - The **other dragons aligned to kill the Yellow and Grey homunculi** in an epic conclusion, in the style of *The Lord of the Rings*.
  - **Afterwards,** Yellow and Grey dragonborn continue to be born, but they **align with the Gold and Silver dragons**.
  - There are **rumours of true Gold and Silver dragonborn** in later eras, but the information is patchy.
  - **To do:** choose which epoch this happened in. Perhaps the one just before the present.
  - *(Confirmed by Troy: "yellow and silver" was a typo for Yellow and Grey.)*

## 3. Session notes

### 2026-10-31, voice, drive to the grocery store

Worked backwards from the present. Everything here is Troy's, unless marked *(Claude)*.

**Epoch 6 is the present day.**

**Epoch 5: the homunculi war.**
- Ended about **two centuries ago**. Since dragonborn can live on by phasing, there are dragonborn **who remember it**: veterans, perhaps in a different life and colour.
- *(Claude, confirmed by Troy)* Young Black's homunculi were a **loophole** in the council's ban on mass phasing (see epoch 4): converting Gold and Silver dragonborn wasn't strictly raising an army by phasing.
- The catastrophe: a war between god-dragons is a true catastrophe in itself. *(Claude's idea, which Troy liked; parked)*: killing the homunculi **released something**.
- **Parked:** whether today's Yellow and Grey dragonborn carry a **stigma** from the war.

**Epoch 4: the twins, and the origin of Brown and Indigo.**
1. **The tryst:** Blue and Green, neighbours on the wheel and the two dragons who get along best, have a tryst. **Two dragon eggs** result, the first eggs any of the dragons has ever seen.
2. **Unphased twins:** the eggs hatch into **Unphased dragons**. Everyone expects them to settle as Blue and Green.
3. **The others interfere:** they fear two Blues and two Greens would unbalance the five worldly dragons.
   - **Black** tries to sway a twin to Black, because more Black is better.
   - **White** tries too, defensively, for fear that Black will succeed.
4. **Black succeeds:** one twin phases **Black**, making two Black dragons. The rift between the twins, and the others' fear of two Blacks, makes the other twin phase **Red**.
   - The new Red is a **different, militant shade**, perhaps crimson. The elder Red is industrial, a maker rather than a warrior.
5. **The siblings' war:** young Black and young Red fight an enormous war.
   - **Mass phasing to raise armies:** dragonborn phase in great numbers into the young Red's shade to fight, and young Black convinces many dragonborn to phase into its own black.
   - **Weapons:** Black, being clever, makes its own weapons. Nobody had to sell them. Some were so dangerous that the elder Black later took the knowledge with it (6).
   - *(Troy)* Not every epoch should be everyone against Black, which would be repetitive. Here it's a war between siblings.
6. **The war ends** when the **elder Black** realizes the disaster is partly its fault and takes responsibility.
   - It phases into **Indigo**, becoming the elder Indigo dragon. The young Black is left as *the* Black dragon.
   - It **takes the knowledge and secrets of the war's worst weapons with it**. *(Claude)* That fits Indigo dealing in secrets and forbidden things, and gives an adventure hook if those secrets ever surface.
7. **The young Red phases into Brown,** against expectations; everyone thought Blue or Green, or something in between. It's a **swords-into-ploughshares** change: from now on, rest, humility, humbleness and peace instead of war.
8. **The first phasing of adult dragons:** epoch 4 is the first time fully grown, god-like dragons ever changed colour.
9. **The council:** afterwards, the dragons form **something like a council of the gods**, to stop mass phasing to raise armies from ever happening again.

**Consequences for the present (epoch 6):**
- **Today's Black dragon is not the original.** It's the young Black from epoch 4, still trying to wrest power from the others, which is why it made the homunculi. A ready-made political rivalry for the current era.
- **The council fell apart** after the homunculi war. What's left is **informal**, with no grand name: an association of **Green, White and Indigo** who keep meeting.
  - **Stays out:** **Brown** (true neutrality), plus **Red** and **Blue** (commerce; they sell to all comers).
  - **Absconded:** young Black.
  - **Above it all:** Gold and Silver, aloof as ever.
- *(Claude)* Mass phasing ties into the phasing brainstorm (`canon/brainstorm-phasing.md`), as both a weapon of war and something the gods have banned.

**Parked:** the map (no geography yet), the Yellow and Grey stigma, and what killing the homunculi released.

### 2026-10-31, voice, drive home from the grocery store

Everything here is Troy's, unless marked *(Claude)*.

**Epoch 2: the machine war** (magic against technology).
- *(Troy)* D&D and Final Fantasy disasters are often about magic against technology. It's a way to scatter **technological artifacts** around a world that's largely magical today: there was a big conflict, and magic came out on top.
- **The machines may be older than the ring.** Machines might already have existed in epoch 1, on the first world. If so, epoch 2 isn't their invention but a **return**. *(Claude)* The myth says everything Azhi ate flowed back out of its wounds, so first-world machines could have come out of the corpse as half-understood relics.
- **Alignments** (before any council or peace):
  - **For the machines:** **Red and Blue**. *(Claude)* Red builds; Blue's ships and ports trade.
  - **Against:**
    - **Green**, for ecological reasons.
    - **Black**, but **for selfish reasons**. *(Claude)* Black gave mortals magic "so that we may be worshipped", so machines that work without magic threaten the worship.
    - **White**, the colour most likely to reject technology outright. It's the ascetics in their monasteries, preserving the status quo.
- **The end: a singularity.**
  - The machines wake. *(Claude)* A thinking engine outgrows its makers and inherits **Azhi's hunger**, consuming the land to build more of itself. That's the **ecological catastrophe**.
  - **Red and Blue flip** to fight their own creation, so all five dragons fight together.
  - The dragons **convince it to eat itself**, beating it with Azhi's own end.
- **Aftermath:**
  - **Lots of little machine fragments are scattered all over the world,** largely **forbidden**.
  - One of **White's monastic missions** is to track these artifacts down and remove them from circulation. *(Claude)* That gives White adventurers a job, and smugglers a profit.

**Epoch 3: the age of faith.**
- **The rise of religion** as a concept takes over the whole civilization, with **wars between religions**. It's not only Black seeking followers.
  - **Blue's religious hucksters** create competing religions: a **bazaar of ideas**.
  - The churches **aren't necessarily run by the dragons**, except perhaps the Black church.
- **The end: a magical apocalypse.**
  - Dragonborn worshippers learn to **tap the dragons' own power through their worship**. The churches overreach, and the result is a magical apocalypse.
  - It **hurt the dragons a little**: **their powers today are less than at the creation of the world**.
  - The dragons step in to **turn off the taps**, so their dragonborn can't draw that power again.
- **The 9th-level cap** *(Troy: "I think this is going to be a rule")*:
  - The churches reached spells **above 9th level** by drawing on the dragons.
  - Afterwards, all spells above 9th level were **blocked**. This gives the game's cap an in-world reason.
  - It borrows the concept only from D&D's Karsus's Folly in Netheril, where a mortal briefly became a god and magic was capped. None of its wording is used.

**Where the outline stands:**
1. **Epoch 1:** the first world, eaten by Azhi.
2. **Epoch 2:** the machine war, ending in a singularity that ate itself.
3. **Epoch 3:** the age of faith, ending in a magical apocalypse and the 9th-level cap.
4. **Epoch 4:** the twins, the origin of Brown and Indigo, and the council against mass phasing.
5. **Epoch 5:** the homunculi war, two centuries ago.
6. **Epoch 6:** the present day.

### 2026-10-31, text

**Epoch 1 had dragonborn** *(Troy)*. They all died at the end of the epoch, when Azhi ate the world, and were **reborn at the start of epoch 2**, when the ring formed. This matches the myth: "all life springs forth anew".

- *(Claude)* This makes the first great **mass rebirth** in the world's history: the whole people coming back at once. It's a natural root for the phasing lore, and for any faith built around rebirth.
