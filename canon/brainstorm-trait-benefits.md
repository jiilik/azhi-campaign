# Brainstorm: Personality Trait Benefits

**Status: PROPOSED** (opened 2026-10-26). This file is the working record for writing the benefits of the 24 personality traits in `srd/04-character-origins/01-personality-traits.md`. Nothing here is canon until Troy approves it.

## 1. Strategy (Troy, 2026-10-26)

- **Strength:** a trait's benefit should be **not as strong as a feat, but nearly**.
  - On the tentative scale, a trait is worth about 10 points, and its +1 ability is 4, so the benefit is about **6 points**, or about **7** for a ★ trait. An Origin feat is 8.
- **Borrow freely from the SRD species.** The dragonborn wheel replaced all nine SRD species, so their traits are spare parts. The same goes for the SRD feats and backgrounds.
  - It's like putting a puzzle back together from pieces to make a new picture entirely: *the SRD phasing.*
- **Third-party material:** borrow concepts from any species, feats or backgrounds we have access to, but **never reuse their wording**, to avoid copyright issues.
- **Aim:** quick placeholder benefits that don't get repetitive, to be refined later.

## 3. First-pass placeholders (PROPOSED, by Claude)

Each benefit is a short sketch, with the parts it reuses and a rough point value (target about 6, or about 7 for ★). "PB" is the Proficiency Bonus.

1. **The Bold** (Str/Dex). Add your PB to Initiative rolls. *(Alert, first half.)* ~6
2. **The Commander** (Str/Cha). Once per Long Rest, as a Bonus Action, give an ally within 30 feet Heroic Inspiration. Proficiency in Intimidation or Persuasion. *(Human Resourceful, given away.)* ~6
3. **The Confidant** (Wis/Cha). Proficiency in Insight. After a minute of conversation, you have Advantage on Insight checks against that creature. *(Elf Keen Senses, focused.)* ~5.5
4. **The Curious** (Dex/Int). Proficiency in two of Investigation, Perception, Sleight of Hand or Thieves' Tools. Advantage on checks to find traps and secret doors. *(Skilled, narrowed.)* ~6
5. **The Debater** (Cha/Int). Proficiency in Deception or Persuasion. *Point of Order:* PB times per Long Rest, as a Reaction, impose Disadvantage on a Charisma check made against you or an ally. *(New; the reverse of Luck.)* ~6
6. **The Devout ★** (Wis). You know *Guidance* and *Thaumaturgy* (Wisdom). Proficiency in Religion. *(Tiefling Otherworldly Presence; Acolyte.)* ~7
7. **The Disciplined** (Str/Con). Proficiency in Athletics. *Muscle Memory:* once per Short Rest, when you make a check with a proficient skill or tool, treat a d20 roll of 9 or lower as a 10. *(New; a narrow reliable-practice feature.)* ~6
8. **The Dreamer ★** (Wis). You gain Heroic Inspiration when you finish a Long Rest. Advantage on saves to avoid acting against your stated convictions *(DM's call)*. *(Human Resourceful.)* ~7
9. **The Generous** (Cha/Con). Proficiency with Cook's Utensils. When you share a meal during a Short Rest, each ally who spends Hit Dice regains extra HP equal to your PB. *(Concept: a cook's feat.)* ~6
10. **The Gentle Giant** (Str/Wis). Advantage on checks to end the Grappled condition, and you count as one size larger when working out your carrying capacity. Proficiency in Animal Handling. *(Goliath Powerful Build.)* ~6
11. **The Guardian** (Con/Str). PB times per Long Rest, as a Reaction when a creature you can see attacks an ally within 5 feet of you, impose Disadvantage on the attack. *(Concept: a protection fighting style; clear of the Bodyguard maneuver.)* ~6.5
12. **The Hearty ★** (Con). Resistance to Poison damage and Advantage on saves against Poisoned. Your HP maximum increases by 1 per level. *(Dwarven Resilience and Dwarven Toughness.)* ~7.5
13. **The Magnificent** (Cha/Dex). Proficiency in Performance. *Spotlight:* once per Long Rest, while you perform, creatures watching you have Disadvantage on Perception checks to notice anything else. *(New; a distraction for stealthy allies.)* ~6
14. **The Mentor** (Cha/Wis). *Lessons:* when you finish a Long Rest, choose an ally and one of your skills. Until their next Long Rest, they count as proficient in it. *(Skilled, lent out.)* ~6
15. **The Prudent** (Wis/Int). Proficiency in Perception. *Packed for It:* once per Long Rest, as a Bonus Action, pull out an ordinary item worth up to 10 GP that you "packed earlier". *(Concept: a well-prepared pack.)* ~6
16. **The Resourceful** (Wis/Dex). Proficiency in Survival and one tool. You can use improvised tools without penalty. *(Concept: Rock Gnome tinkering.)* ~5.5
17. **The Restless ★** (Dex). PB times per Long Rest, take the Dash action as a Bonus Action and gain Temporary HP equal to your PB. *(Orc Adrenaline Rush.)* ~7
18. **The Rock** (Con/Str). You can't be Surprised, and you have Advantage on saves against Frightened. *(Alert concept; Halfling Brave.)* ~6
19. **The Scrapper** (Str/Dex). Your Unarmed Strikes deal 1d4 + Str, and once per turn a hit can push the target 5 feet. Proficiency with improvised weapons. *(Concept: a tavern-brawler feat.)* ~6
20. **The Steward** (Int/Con). Proficiency with one tool. Your group can travel at a Fast pace without its Disadvantage on Perception, Survival and Stealth checks. You can keep watch and still gain a Long Rest's benefits. *(Trance concept, for the group.)* ~6
21. **The Strategist ★** (Int). When you roll Initiative, you can swap results with a willing ally. Add your Intelligence modifier to Initiative instead of Dexterity. *(Alert, second half.)* ~7
22. **The Theorist ★** (Int). You know *Mending* and *Prestidigitation* (Intelligence), and you can build tiny clockwork devices. *(Rock Gnome.)* ~7
23. **The Unbound ★** (Cha). Advantage on saves against Charmed. Once per Long Rest, you can cast *Misty Step* to slip free. *(Elf Fey Ancestry; High Elf lineage.)* ~7
24. **The Wanderer** (Con/Dex). Your Speed increases by 5 feet. You can cast *Longstrider* on yourself once per Long Rest. Proficiency in Survival. *(Wood Elf lineage.)* ~6

**Notes:**
- **Alert is split** between the Bold (the Initiative bonus) and the Strategist (the swap).
- **Human Resourceful** is used three ways: kept (the Dreamer), given away (the Commander) and lent out as a skill (the Mentor).
- **Overlap to watch:**
  - The Resourceful and the Wanderer both give Survival.
  - The Bold and the Strategist both affect Initiative.
  - The Guardian and the Debater both use a Reaction-based Disadvantage.

## 4. Troy's feedback on the first pass (2026-10-26)

- **Static over resources.** Too many benefits reset on a Long Rest. Prefer static benefits with nothing to track. For example, the Guardian: "use your Reaction to take half the damage dealt to an adjacent ally".
- **10, the Gentle Giant:** rejected. Powerful Build would stack dramatically with Red (Large, Mighty Frame), and Troy doesn't want to carve out an exception.
- **8, the Dreamer:** try *Minor Illusion* as a Bonus Action ("dreams leaking into reality"). Is it too strong?
- **14, the Mentor:** it only works if the character has lots of skill proficiencies, so add a proficiency in a skill or tool of their choice.
- **15, the Prudent:** "Really like it," like a robe of useful items. The flavour is great, but the mechanics need work: the character mustn't be able to sell 10 GP of goods every day.
- **17, the Restless:** would a catnap-style benefit (a Short Rest's benefits in 10 minutes) be better inspiration?
- **20, the Steward:** the Fast-pace travel is too game-altering, especially at level 1. The Long Rest watch doesn't match the flavour text. Needs something else.

## 6. Third pass, by voice (2026-10-27, morning commute)

*(Troy)* Everything in this pass is **tentative**; there will be more passes. A trait should have something for combat **and** something outside it. Rule of thumb from this session: stay on a trait until Troy says it's done.

### Tentatively settled

1. **The Bold** (Str/Dex)
   - Add your PB to Initiative.
   - *First Through the Door:* when you volunteer to go first into something risky, such as a jump, a duel or a confrontation, you have Advantage on that first check.
   - *(Troy)* Chose this over Intimidation or Athletics, because it rewards playing the way the description reads.
2. **The Commander** (Str/Cha)
   - Proficiency in Intimidation or Persuasion.
   - *Follow Me:* allies within 30 feet who can see or hear you have Advantage on saves against Frightened.
   - *Chain of Command:* soldiers, guards and militia recognize you as one of their own, so you can always get a hearing with whoever's in charge of them. *(Troy)* A martial version of Indigo's underworld door-opener; it doesn't stomp on anything.
   - *(Troy)* Bold plus Commander is a fun combo: "Follow me!", first through the door, and everyone follows. The Commander leads from the front; planning in the tent is the Strategist's job.
   - **Rejected:**
     - *Take Charge*, a group-check reroll: too much like Bardic Inspiration.
     - *Voice of Authority*, Advantage when giving orders.
     - *Rally*, Temporary HP on Initiative: the trait needed something outside combat.
3. **The Confidant** (Wis/Cha), with Claude's reading of the wording approved by Troy:
   - **Skill:** you have proficiency in Insight.
   - **Keeper of Confidences:** when a person, place or faction comes up, you can make an Intelligence check to recall something someone once told you about it in confidence, such as a secret, a rumour or a quiet grudge. The DM supplies the detail.
   - **Unspoken Signals:** you know the *Message* cantrip. For you it takes no whisper, just a raised eyebrow or a tap of the nose that your companions understand.
   - *(Troy)* The Confidant is someone people spill the beans to, not a lie detector. Think butlers, bartenders and priests (Alfred, Guinan, Friar Laurence); attorney-client privilege. It stacks well in flavour with Grey (rumours plus secrets) and Indigo (a spymaster).
   - Rough value: Insight ~2, *Keeper of Confidences* ~2, a utility cantrip ~2, so ~6.
   - **Rejected:**
     - Advantage after a minute of talk: too situational, and it forces a minute of conversation.
     - *Read the Tell*: Advantage on saves against a studied creature.
     - *Something Unsaid*: every conversation would end in "are they holding back?", which is repetitive at the table.
     - A +5 to Passive Insight: that's a lie detector, not a confidant.
   - **Unused:** *Trusted Ear*, Advantage on Charisma checks to get someone to open up.
4. **The Curious** (Dex/Int)
   - Proficiency in two skills or tools of your choice, with no list. *(Troy)* The Curious should be able to be a skill monkey. Curious plus Mentor is a natural pairing, the way a Mentor picks up skills to teach.
   - Advantage on checks to find traps and secret doors. *(Troy)* Strong in exploration, and exploration characters will want it.
   - *Poke It and See:* the first time you hit a creature, you learn one of its resistances, immunities or vulnerabilities, if it has any.
   - Stacking check: a Grey Curious, with Bonus Action Search plus this Advantage, finds nearly every trap. A rogue or bard Curious gets two more skills for Expertise. Both are strong, not broken.

### Parked in the parts bin

- **Read Their Intent:** as a Bonus Action, make an Insight check against a creature you can see. On a success, the DM tells you what it means to do next, such as who it's targeting or whether it's about to flee, and you can call it out to your allies. No other mechanical edge. *(Troy)* A good idea, but not a confidant: it's reading the body language of combat foes. **Tentatively moved to the Prudent**, to be revisited there.

### Paused mid-trait: the Debater (Cha/Int)

- Tentative: proficiency in **both** Deception and Persuasion.
- *Point of Order* is on hold. *(Troy)* Freely imposing Disadvantage on anyone's Charisma check seems strong. It barely matters in combat, but it does in turn-based social scenes, which some DMs run.
- Claude's suggested fix, not yet discussed: only on a Charisma check made to persuade, deceive or intimidate you or an ally within earshot; you have to speak up and interrupt, so everyone knows you did it.
- **Resume here next time,** then continue from the Devout (6).

## 7. Fourth pass, by voice (2026-10-27, evening commute)

Still **tentative**. One new rule from Troy:

- **No Heroic Inspiration in any trait.** The flaws will provide it for anyone who takes one, and a character can hold only one at a time.

### Tentatively settled

5. **The Debater** (Cha/Int)
   - Proficiency in **Deception and Persuasion**.
   - *Riposte:* when you fail a Persuasion or Deception check, you can immediately try again with the other skill, switching from honest argument to clever spin or back. You must use the new roll. *(Troy)* The name is a fencing term: duelling with words.
   - *Banter:* as a Bonus Action, you trade quips with a creature that can hear you and understands your language. Make a Persuasion or Deception check against its Insight. If you win, it's too busy thinking of a comeback to take Reactions until your next turn.
     - *(Troy)* Inspired by the sword fight in *The Princess Bride*. A shared language is enough; there's no Intelligence minimum.
   - **Rejected:**
     - *Point of Order*, a Reaction Disadvantage on Charisma checks: too strong as a free veto.
     - *Riposte* triggered by an NPC's failed check: DMs rarely roll Charisma checks for NPCs, so the player should trigger it.
6. **The Devout ★** (Wis)
   - You know *Thaumaturgy*.
   - Proficiency in one of Religion, History or Arcana, so a Black or Grey Devout who already has Religion isn't wasted.
   - *Steadfast Faith:* when you, or an ally within 30 feet that you can see, makes a death saving throw, add your Wisdom modifier. *(Troy)* A remote boost to death saves, without handing out *Spare the Dying*.
   - *Faithful Welcome:* at any shrine or temple of your faith, you and your companions can find free food, lodging and basic healing, and the clergy will hear you out.
     - *(Troy)* It stacks in fun with the Commander's *Chain of Command* and Indigo's network: walk into a town and settle in. Those are role-play door-openers, not power.
   - **Dropped:** *Guidance*. *(Troy)* Too easy a way to pick up *Guidance* from outside a class's spell list; keep *Thaumaturgy* instead.
7. **The Disciplined** (Str/Con)
   - Proficiency in **one skill or tool of your choice**.
   - *Muscle Memory:* choose one skill or tool you're proficient in. Whenever you make a check with it, roll a d6 and add the number rolled. The die becomes a d8 at level 5, a d10 at level 11 and a d12 at level 17. To change your chosen skill or tool, you must practise the new one for seven Long Rests in a row.
     - *(Troy)* Players like rolling dice. The level steps are Troy's, with Claude's suggestion of 17 rather than 16. Long Rests replace days, since Azhi has no steady day.
     - Combat use depends on the chosen skill, such as Athletics or Acrobatics against grapples, or Stealth for a rogue.
   - *(Troy)* It's open to any class: a rogue, or a wizard drilling Arcana with a Constitution boost. Constitution will be the common pick.
   - Value: the skill ~2 plus the scaling die ~4, so ~6.
   - **Rejected:**
     - A minimum roll of 10: too strong, since a lockpicker would open every door forever.
     - Advantage on a retry: DMs often forbid retries, such as for locks.
     - A minimum roll of 5.
     - Two free skills: over budget, and it would rival the Curious as the skill monkey.
8. **The Dreamer ★** (Wis), an introvert (INFP), so all Wisdom with no Charisma or performance.
   - *Prophetic Dreams:* before a Long Rest, you can ask the DM a question about something that troubles you. You dream an answer that's cryptic, symbolic and never wrong, but always open to interpretation.
     - *(Troy)* A way for the DM to give the party soft hooks ("I just dreamt about something weird"). The DM can be as wishy-washy as needed.
   - Proficiency in one of Insight, Animal Handling, Painter's Supplies or Calligrapher's Supplies.
   - *Déjà Vu:* once per Short or Long Rest, you can act on a half-remembered dream. Either use your Reaction to give an attack against you Disadvantage, or give yourself Advantage on an attack roll you make.
     - *(Troy)* Not "once per fight", which feels like 4th edition encounter powers.
   - Value ~7.
   - **Rejected:**
     - *Minor Illusion* as a Bonus Action: the 2024 Illusionist wizard already does this (not SRD), so that subclass would lose out.
     - *Hope Springs Eternal* (Heroic Inspiration on a Long Rest): see the rule above.
     - *Augury*, as a Ritual or in a dream: too limited for hooks, and the 30-minute window is lame.

### Parked in the parts bin

- **Lingering Illusion:** your *Minor Illusion* lasts until you dismiss it or cast it again, one at a time. *(Troy)* "I love the feature," but it doesn't fit the Dreamer.

### Paused: the Generous (Cha/Con)

- *(Troy)* Too cooking-oriented and too Short Rest-oriented. It's too like a cook feat, and should be about the personality rather than the cooking. Cook's Utensils and the Hit Dice bonus are on hold.
- **Rejected:** *Goodwill*, Advantage on Charisma checks with anyone you've given a gift or favour.
- **Resume here next time,** then continue from the Gentle Giant (10).

## 8. Fifth pass, by voice (2026-10-28, morning commute)

Still **tentative**.

### Tentatively settled

9. **The Generous** (Cha/Con)
   - *(Troy)* Benevolence, not cooking. Reward the player for playing generously, with nothing for the DM to track, nothing delayed, and no money engine.
   - You know *Guidance*. This is the **only** trait that grants *Guidance* outside a class's list. *(Troy)* It's the giving piece, and it's underrated as a cantrip: value ~3, not 2.5. A fighter with *Guidance* will spam it.
   - *After You:* when an ally within 30 feet that you can see would take a Reaction but has already used theirs, you can spend your Reaction instead to let them take it.
     - Examples: a fighter's second opportunity attack, or a wizard's *Shield* and *Counterspell* in one round. Two *Shield*s do nothing, since it lasts until the wizard's next turn.
     - *(Troy)* Strong but situational: rare, and memorable when it happens. It's a little 4e Warlord or Battle Master in a trait, for a battlefield-control build, and it pairs with the Commander.
   - *The Shirt off Your Back:* when you spend Hit Dice during a Short Rest, you can give any of them to an ally instead, who rolls them and heals.
     - Re-added by Troy after first worrying that it made generosity a healing thing; a bard or cleric will stack it well.
   - Value: *Guidance* ~3, *After You* ~2, Hit Dice ~2, so ~7. *(Troy)* A touch over target is fine for three situational pieces. "Really fun to play."
   - **Rejected:**
     - *Open Purse*, extra coin: anything renewable becomes a money engine.
     - *Kindness Returned*, the DM returns kindnesses later: the DM has to track it.
     - *Warm Heart*, Temporary HP when you give something to a creature in need: set aside.
     - *Goodwill*: rejected last pass.
     - The cooking pieces.

## 9. Sixth pass, by voice (2026-10-28, evening), and where the Magnificent came from

### The Magnificent (13), in progress
- **Skill choice, tentative:** proficiency in one of **Performance, Acrobatics, Sleight of Hand, a musical instrument, a Disguise Kit or Painter's Supplies**.
  - *(Troy)* Think of the people called magnificent: circus performers, musicians, anyone who can go on and dazzle an audience, and sometimes distract it in the bread-and-circuses sense rather than the mechanical one.
  - Glassblower's Tools were rejected as more blue-collar, hands-on art.
- *Spotlight* (as a Bonus Action, creatures of your choice within 30 feet that see you have Disadvantage on Perception checks to notice anything else): *(Troy)* feels a bit mechanically complex. He's thinking about it.
- **Candidates raised:** *Minor Illusion* (no trait grants it now), perhaps with *Lingering Illusion* (§7, parked). *Prestidigitation* is already the Theorist's.
- **To do:** revisit the Dreamer's Painter's Supplies, now that the Magnificent offers them too.
- *(Troy)* Paused to dig into where the Magnificent came from, so it **feels like a personality trait and not just a title**.

### Lineage (from `canon/brainstorm-personality.md`)
- **Type:** ESFP, in the "living in the moment" group, next to the Resourceful (ISTP), the Wanderer (ISFP) and the Bold (ESTP). ESFP is 16Personalities' *Entertainer* and Keirsey's *Performer*.
- **First name:** *the Showman*, "lights up the room", invented in the voice session as one of 16 everyday archetypes (§4 there).
- **Forerunner:** the old canon trait **Earworm** ("everyone remembers your songs"), which mapped to the Showman.
- **Renamed** *the Magnificent* because *Showman* isn't gender-neutral. *The Dazzling* was rejected: *(Troy)* "sounds like a stage magician".
- **Core idea:** "wins people over by presence; attention follows them." *(Troy, 2026-10-24)* "the very pretty character". The Braggart, now a flaw, *asks* for the spotlight; the Magnificent never needs to.
- **Seeds at drafting:** first impressions and winning over a crowd. Keep it clear of the Commander (the party face who gives direction) and the Generous (hospitality).

### What the name carries
- **The historical epithet:** *Lorenzo the Magnificent* (de' Medici) and *Suleiman the Magnificent* were famed for splendour: festivals, art, display and grand public works. "Magnificence" was a virtue of lavish public spending.
  - That edges toward the Generous (giving) and the Commander (rule). The Magnificent's share is the **splendour and spectacle**, not the giving.
- **The ESFP side:** spontaneous, playful and sociable, at home in the senses (colour, music, food, fashion), and happiest with an audience. Easily bored, and avoids conflict by charming its way around it.

So the Magnificent's personality is **charisma as presence and spectacle**: people warm to you on sight, and a crowd turns to watch. It isn't giving orders (the Commander) or arguing (the Debater).

## 16. Balance pass, part 1, by voice (2026-10-30, morning commute)

Reviewing duplicates, clashes and broken combos across forms and traits. Verdicts are Troy's.

1. **+3 to one ability: allowed.** Two traits plus a form can all raise one ability. With the standard array that's 18 at level 1, which is under the cap of 20.
2. **Reaction crowding: fine.** Seven traits use the Reaction. *(Troy)* Reactions are under-used in the base game; most characters only ever have the standard ones. Picking two Reaction traits is a real choice.
3. **The Help engine: approved.** A White Steward Helps with its Bonus Action and again with its Reaction, so two allies, or both of one fighter's attacks, get Advantage.
   - Limits:
     - Helping an attack needs the enemy within 5 feet of you, so it's a front-line move, not a back-row one.
     - Advantage doesn't stack.
     - It costs the Bonus Action and Reaction, so no opportunity attacks that round.
     - It only pays off when an ally attacks the same enemy.
   - Gamed out:
     - **A paladin Steward with a rogue:** Sneak Attack already applies, so the Help only adds accuracy.
     - **A Valor bard Steward:** Bardic Inspiration competes for the Bonus Action. Valor isn't in the SRD; it was a thought experiment.
     - **A two-weapon ranger Steward:** its Bonus Action is already crowded.
     - **Strongest case, a fighter Steward beside a paladin:** strong, about feat level, but fair.
4. **Stacked rolls: approved.**
   - **Level 1:** *Muscle Memory*, Help, *Guidance* and *Harebrained Scheme* on one Stealth check average about 28.
   - **Level 17:** about 39, maximum 52. The hardest DC is 30.
   - It takes four characters and their resources, and *Guidance* needs Concentration.
   - *(Troy)* A party built to break this sounds fun, but it's rare. With 10–11 trait picks in a party of five, the chance of all four traits appearing is about 3% if picks were random.
5. **Initiative: as is.** A Bold Strategist wizard almost always goes first. *(Troy)* That's what the player bought with both traits: a battlefield controller extraordinaire. The swap can hand the top spot to an ally.
6. **Overlaps:**
   - **The Unbound and Black's *Guarded Mind*:** accepted. A Charisma-chasing Black Unbound is a munchkin build and takes the small loss.
   - **The Commander's *Follow Me* and the Gentle Giant's *Soothing Presence*:** approved. One is a ranged rallying cry against fear; the other is an action-and-touch cure, and the only one that ends Charmed (for example, a vampire's thrall). They complement each other.
   - **Improvised weapons on both the Resourceful and the Scrapper:** approved. The DM can already treat an improvised weapon as a similar real weapon (a table leg as a club), so the proficiency is mostly flavour. A Resourceful Scrapper is Jackie Chan.
   - **Survival on five lists** (Resourceful, Wanderer, Strategist, Brown and Green): kept. It's a good adventurer skill, and a party should have one or two characters with it. Only Green grants it outright; the rest are choices.
