# Design Review Checklist

Checks to run against the Azhi design at any time: as a preliminary analysis while the design is in progress, and as the final systems check once it's done. When Troy asks for a review, go through each check against the current repo (the setting bible, the decision log, the canon specs and the `srd/` files). Report each as **pass**, **issue** (`file · problem · suggested fix`) or **not yet testable**, then add a line to the review log at the end.

**Only canon goes on this list.** Ideas still under brainstorming are added only once Troy approves them into canon. Examples are the one-flaw cap and trait exclusions in `canon/brainstorm-personality.md`.

| # | Check | Passes when | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Canonical builds** | Every class has two or three workable builds (a form plus personality traits), and no class is left without a form or traits that suit it. This is checked once the classes are settled; builds aren't written in advance. | Troy, 2026-10-23 |
| 2 | **Point budget** | Each form and trait sits within the tentative point scale, and a full character totals **about 35** points (affinity about 16, about 10 per trait). The colours' 17–18.5 and the hybrids' 13–16 were accepted. | `canon/decisions.md`, 2026-10-21 |
| 3 | **No racist dragons** | No virtue, flaw or worth is tied to a colour. Differences between colours are elemental opposition or policy disputes, never superiority or prejudice. | Standing rule; `canon/setting-bible.md` §3 |
| 4 | **Dragonborn only** | No other sentient species appears, in text or in art. The dragons themselves and non-sentient creatures are the only exceptions. | Standing rule; `canon/setting-bible.md` §3 |
| 5 | **Choice overload** | No step of character creation offers so many options at once that a new player stalls ("paralysis by analysis"). | Troy, 2026-10-23 |
| 6 | **Rules hygiene** | Rules come from SRD 5.2.1 only, with no 2014 / SRD 5.1 rules unless Troy asked. The attribution statement is kept in any compiled document. Spelling is Canadian. | Standing rules; `ATTRIBUTION.md` |
| 7 | **Signature features** | Darkvision belongs to Black alone. No form feature is a combat ability a build must be designed around. Features fit the elemental wheel. Swapping belongs only to the Unphased. Swim, climb and fly speeds equal walking speed. Every form has two ribbons. | `canon/decisions.md`, 2026-10-21 |
