# Proposed Changes to the SRD Monster List

**Status: PROPOSED.** This is a running list of changes to the SRD 5.2.1 monsters (`srd/11-monsters`, `srd/12-monsters-a-z`, `srd/13-animals`), with the reason for each. Nothing is applied yet. If Troy accepts the list, it gets applied in one pass and logged in `canon/decisions.md`.

Each entry has a number, the change, the monsters it affects, and why. Background: `canon/audit-planes.md` and the planes notes in `canon/brainstorm-epochs.md`.

## A. Removals

**A1. Remove all Fiends (29).** *(Troy, 2026-11-01)*
- **Why:** the Outer Planes are scrapped entirely, so there are no hells or abysses for fiends to come from.
- **Affected:** Balor, Barbed Devil, Bearded Devil, Bone Devil, Chain Devil, Dretch, Erinyes, Glabrezu, Gnoll Warrior, Hell Hound, Hezrou, Horned Devil, Ice Devil, Imp, Incubus, Lamia, Lemure, Marilith, Nalfeshnee, Night Hag, Nightmare, Oni, Pit Fiend, Quasit, Rakshasa, Sahuagin Warrior, Spirit Naga, Succubus, Vrock.
- **Note:** Gnoll Warrior and Sahuagin Warrior are peoples too, so they'd also go under the dragonborn-only rule.

**A2. Remove all Celestials except the three giant animals (10).** *(Troy, 2026-11-01)*
- **Why:** with no Outer Planes, there are no Upper Planes for celestials to come from.
- **Affected:** Couatl, Deva, Guardian Naga, Pegasus, Planetar, Solar, Sphinx of Lore, Sphinx of Valor, Sphinx of Wonder, Unicorn.
- **Kept and changed:** Giant Eagle, Giant Elk and Giant Owl (see B1).

## B. Changes

**B1. Giant Eagle, Giant Elk and Giant Owl become Beasts.** *(Troy, 2026-11-01)*
- **Type:** Celestial → **Beast**, the same type as the ordinary Eagle, Elk and Owl.
- **Why:** there are no celestials, and these are just big animals on Azhi.
- **Knock-on fixes**, PROPOSED by Claude for Troy to confirm, since the type alone leaves celestial traits behind:
  - **a. Alignment:** Neutral Good (Eagle, Elk) and Neutral (Owl) → **Unaligned**, like their ordinary versions.
  - **b. Resistances:** remove Necrotic and Radiant (all three).
  - **c. Radiant damage riders:** remove the extra Radiant damage on the Giant Eagle's Rend and the Giant Elk's Ram. That lowers their damage a little, so their CR (1 and 2) may need a check.
  - **d. Languages:** Celestial, plus understanding Common, Primordial, Elvish or Sylvan → **None**, like the ordinary animals. Or keep "understands Common but can't speak it", if Troy wants them to be smart animals.
  - **e. The Giant Owl's Spellcasting** (*Detect Evil and Good*, *Detect Magic*, *Clairvoyance*): remove it, or keep it as an uncanny wise owl.

## C. Knock-on effects outside the monster list (noted, not proposed here)

Scrapping the Outer Planes also touches the following. All are listed in full in `canon/audit-planes.md` §4. They need their own decisions when spells, classes and items are revised.
- **Classes:** the cleric's and paladin's Channel Divinity ("from the Outer Planes"); the warlock's Fiend Patron, the only warlock subclass in the SRD.
- **Rules:** the Dead condition (the spirit "departed for the Outer Planes").
- **Spells:** *Conjure Celestial*, *Planar Ally*, *Divine Word*, *Find Familiar* and *Find Steed* forms, the *Protection from Evil and Good* family.
- **Items and equipment:** Holy Water, *Candle of Invocation*, *Holy Avenger*, *Mace of Disruption*, the two talismans.
- **Languages:** Abyssal, Celestial and Infernal.
