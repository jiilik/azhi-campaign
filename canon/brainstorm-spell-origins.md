# Brainstorm: Where Each Spell Came From

**Status: PROPOSED, tentative** (opened 2026-11-01). This is a working list of where each SRD spell came from. Most spells were taught by one of the seven source dragons in epoch 2; some were invented later by dragonborn. The background is "the origin of magic" in `canon/brainstorm-epochs.md` (2026-11-01).

Everything here is Troy's, unless marked *(Claude)*. Claude suggests each spell and Troy decides.

## How the list is built

- **A test run** *(Troy, 2026-11-01)*: the first pass was a test of whether the idea works at all. It did, so the list carries on, spell by spell, as a game to play while driving.
- **No balancing** *(Troy)*: don't steer the picks to even out the dragons' lists. Claude's choice of spells might not be random, so let it shake down naturally.
- **The dragons taught up to level 9** *(Troy, 2026-11-04; revised the same evening from "up to level 5")*. So any spell of levels 1–9 can have a teaching dragon. Only spells above level 9 came later, in epoch 3.
- **Not every spell was taught by a dragon.** Some were discovered by dragonborn in later epochs (see "Later discoveries" below).

## Rules of thumb

| Source | Domain |
|---|---|
| **Red** | fire; earth and terrain-altering magic; the smith |
| **Green** | most healing; all plant magic; animals |
| **White** | air (fits *Spectral Wings*); protection and defence; battlefield control; messengers and couriers |
| **Blue** | water; charms and mind control ("overrides"); languages |
| **Black** | force and raw destruction; illusion; knowledge; stealth (later Indigo's speciality) |
| **Gold** | most light and radiant magic; lightning |
| **Silver** | most teleportation; detection |
| **Epoch 3** | all necrotic magic; all shape-changing magic; spells that tap divine power |

## Later discoveries

**Epoch 3, the age of faith:**
- **Magic above level 9** came in epoch 3, the magical apocalypse.
  - *Revised 2026-11-04 (Troy):* the dragons taught up to level 9 in epoch 2, not 5. That settles *Earthquake* (8, Red), *Teleport* (7, Silver) and *Sunbeam* (6, Gold): they stay with their dragons.
  - **Open** *(Claude)*: earlier the same day, studying the machines' escape into their pocket dimension was what took the dragonborn from level 6 to 9. Is that study now what took them past level 9 in epoch 3, or is it dropped?
- *Blight* (necrotic; useless against machines, so never needed in epoch 2)
- *Animate Dead*
- *Polymorph* (shape-changing)
- *Bless* (it taps divine power and feels very religious)
- *Raise Dead*: **tentative**, to revisit with the necrotic and undead lore.

**Epoch 5, the homunculi war:**
- *Wish*: **the one exception.** It wasn't taught by any dragon. A band of dragonborn adventurers found it digging into Azhi's brain, and used it to end the war (see `canon/brainstorm-epochs.md`, 2026-11-01).

**Epoch 6, the present:**
- *Dominate Monster*: discovered in the players' own era, since monsters in the usual sense didn't exist before the homunculi died.

## Questions this raises *(Claude)*

- ~~**Blue's role:** "trader" or "traitor"?~~ **Answered (Troy): trader.**
- **Damage types split between dragons.** Thunder goes to Red (*Thunderwave*) and to Black (*Shatter*), and lightning goes to Gold, not to White's air. So damage type is a guide, not a rule; each spell is decided on its own.
- **The cleric's list.** If spells that tap divine power date from epoch 3, much of the cleric's list may come from there. What did clerics cast before, or did they exist at all?
  - **Answer (Troy):** perhaps **clerics first emerged in epoch 3**, to counter the new school of necromancy.
- **Necrotic damage in older places.** If necrotic damage first appeared in epoch 3, nothing from epochs 1 and 2 (the machine crater, Azhi's brain) should deal it, unless something was added later.
- **Who taught when** *(Troy, 2026-11-04)*: **no dragon taught magic until Red and Blue switched sides** in the machine war. All seven taught after that.
- ***Reincarnate***, ***Power Word: Phase*** **and the other phasing spells** (from the phasing brainstorm) fit the epoch 3 rule on shape-changing too.
- **Other "Monster" spells.** *Hold Monster* would date from epoch 6 if it follows *Dominate Monster*, but the other *Monster* spells could keep their dragons if they're renamed.
