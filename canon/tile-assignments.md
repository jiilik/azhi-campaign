## Working notes: Wild Shape and Vitality *(Troy, voice, 2026-11-15)*

- **The cost fits the thinnest route in.** The cheapest way to Shaman (312) is Tank Kit (no mana), Vitality (208, 2 mana), Shaman (4 mana): **6 mana on arrival**. At 4 mana a shape, and with a Short Rest restoring mana equal to the Proficiency Bonus (2 at first), that's **two Wild Shapes a day**, like the SRD druid's two uses at low level. A Mystic coming straight in has about 12.
- **Duration:** the SRD's "half your druid level in hours" becomes a flat hour, since a higher-level character can simply spend more mana to sustain it.
- **Hit points:** in 2024 the beast has no hit points of its own; you keep yours and the form doesn't end when the Temporary Hit Points run out (unlike 2014). Troy split the Temporary Hit Points out of Wild Shape and onto the Vitality line instead.
- **Vitality's rate:** priced against *False Life* (SRD: 2d4 + 4, about 9, for 2 mana) and *Cure Wounds* (about 12 for 2 mana): roughly 5 per mana early, so **5 / 8 / 12 per mana** on Vitality, II and III. A Bonus Action, so it competes with Wild Shape's Bonus Action; Troy is fine with that.
- **No guaranteed stamina** on the Shaman's routes (Mystic Kit, Tank Kit, Vitality, Mystic II and Shaman are all mana or nothing); only a species colour gives it.
