# The Forbidden Arts

**Status: PROPOSED, brainstorming** (Troy, voice, 2026-11-17). Nothing here is decided. Everything is Troy's unless marked *(Claude)*.

## The idea

- A few tiles that **aren't on the skills grid**: drawn apart from it, in a cloud below the grid, under a heading such as **the Forbidden Arts**.
- They hold everything the **draconic councils have banned**.
- A character learns one only by **special dispensation from the DM**, through the story.
- **Hook:** finishing a line on the grid can lead to a forbidden door. For example, a Summoner who completes their line discovers the banned arts they could reach.
- It also answers two gaps from the archetype review (`canon/brainstorm-progression.md`, "Archetype review"): the **necromancer** and the **tinker**. Neither gets a line on the grid; both live here.

## The list, by epoch

Each banned art comes from the epoch that made it a danger (`canon/brainstorm-epochs.md`).

| Epoch | Banned art | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 2: the machine war | **Tinkering**: the machine age's crafts, such as building thinking machines or mechanical companions | Banned after the machines nearly consumed the world. Machine fragments are already "largely forbidden" (epochs file). |
| 2 | **Extradimensional and planar magic**, such as *Rope Trick* and *Plane Shift* | Pocket dimensions are machine-age technology (epochs file, 2026-10-31). *(Claude: the "thinning boundaries" idea gives a further reason: such magic weakens reality.)* |
| 3: the age of faith | **Necromancy and undead** | The churches raised undead armies: the undead could be controlled as the living couldn't, because **the undead don't phase**. |
| 3 | **Tapping a dragon's power through worship** *(Claude's suggestion; Troy agreed)* | Rival religions doing exactly this caused the epoch's magical apocalypse. The world has been forced into a kind of atheism, even though its gods are alive. *(Claude: this sharpens the open question of where today's Mystics draw their power.)* |
| 3 | **Access to the elemental planes** | The planes have been mostly sealed since epoch 3, with a few imperfect rifts (epochs file). This touches the Summoner, since elementals were a likely source to summon from. |
| 4: the twins' war | **Forced phasing**: making someone phase against their will, for example kidnapping someone into a ritual circle to turn them into a Red warrior because you want one | The siblings' war raised armies by mass phasing. This extends the council's existing ban on mass phasing (epochs file). |
| 5: the homunculi war | **Creating homunculi**, and spells that copy a creature, such as *Clone* and *Simulacrum* *(Claude suggested Simulacrum)* | Black forged false Gold and Silver dragons to control people: about the worst crime against the dragons there is. Homunculi stay servants, never people. |
| 6: the present | None yet | No world-shattering crisis has happened this epoch. |

## Beyond the arts

- ***Wish*** is banned and is never a tile; no player can reach it.
- ***True Polymorph***: either ban it with a lore reason, or drop it from the spell list. *Open.* *(Claude: one reason would be that it's a shortcut to forging false dragons, like the homunculi.)*
- *(Claude)* ***True Resurrection*** cuts across phasing and may need the same treatment. Not discussed.
- The full spell review will sort out exactly which spells fall under each ban (`PLAN.md`, Spells).
