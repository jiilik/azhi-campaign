# Proposed Changes to the SRD Spells

**Status: PROPOSED.** This is a running list of changes to the SRD 5.2.1 spells (`srd/07-spells`), with the reason for each. Nothing is applied yet. If Troy accepts the list, it gets applied in one pass and logged in `canon/decisions.md`. It works the same way as `canon/proposed-monster-changes.md`.

**Who decided what:**
- *(Troy)* marks a change Troy asked for directly.
- *(follows from Troy)* marks Claude's reading of what one of Troy's decisions requires. Troy confirms these.
- *(Claude)* marks Claude's own idea.

**Background:** `canon/audit-planes.md` (§§1–6), the planes notes in `canon/brainstorm-epochs.md`, and `canon/brainstorm-phasing.md`.

**Planes today** *(Troy, 2026-11-01)*:
- **Kept:** the Astral, the Ethereal and the four Elemental Planes (fire, water, earth, air).
- **Scrapped:** the Outer Planes (and so all Celestials and Fiends), the Feywild (and so all Fey) and, by omission, the Shadowfell.

## A. Removals

**A1. *Conjure Celestial*** *(follows from Troy)*. It calls a Celestial from the Upper Planes, and neither exists.

**A2. *Conjure Fey*** *(follows from Troy)*. It calls a Fey spirit from the Feywild, and neither exists.

**A3. *Planar Ally*** *(follows from Troy; or rework, see B9)*. It calls a Celestial, Elemental or Fiend sent by a god or demon prince. Only the Elemental option survives.

## B. Changes

**B1. *Find Familiar*** *(follows from Troy)*. The familiar is "a Celestial, Fey, or Fiend (your choice) instead of a Beast". With none of those types left, it needs a new type, or stays a Beast. Options:
- **a.** It stays a Beast, a spirit in animal form.
- **b.** It becomes an Elemental spirit in animal form.

**B2. *Find Steed*** *(follows from Troy)*. The steed's creature type is "Celestial, Fey, or Fiend", and that choice sets some of its traits. The same options as B1 apply. The traits tied to each type need rewriting.

**B3. *Protection from Evil and Good*, *Magic Circle*, *Detect Evil and Good*, *Hallow*, *Dispel Evil and Good*** *(follows from Troy)*. Their lists of creature types ("Aberrations, Celestials, Elementals, Fey, Fiends, or Undead") drop Celestials, Fey and Fiends.
- *Dispel Evil and Good* also names the Feywild and the Shadowfell, so those references go too.
- Whether Undead stay depends on the open undead question.

**B4. *Divine Word*** *(follows from Troy)*.
- It draws on "power from the Upper Planes", so the source needs rewording, perhaps to the dragons.
- It sends Celestials, Elementals, Fey and Fiends home; only Elementals are left.

**B5. *Divine Smite* and *Holy Aura*** *(follows from Troy)*. Both have extra effects against "a Fiend or an Undead". The Fiend half goes; the Undead half waits on the undead question.

**B6. *Banishment*** *(follows from Troy)*.
- If the target is "an Aberration, a Celestial, an Elemental, a Fey, or a Fiend", it can be sent to its home plane for good. Only Aberrations and Elementals are left.
- Aberrations would need a home "beyond", which ties into the thinning-boundaries idea.
- The demiplane it sends targets to could become a machine-age pocket dimension.

**B7. *Creation*** *(follows from Troy)*. It pulls "shadow material from the Shadowfell". That needs a new source, such as the Ethereal or raw elemental matter.

**B8. *Forbiddance*** *(follows from Troy)*. It blocks travel "by way of the Astral Plane, the Ethereal Plane, the Feywild, the Shadowfell, or the *Plane Shift* spell", and lists Celestials, Elementals, Fey and Fiends. The Feywild, the Shadowfell and the scrapped types go.

**B9. *Planar Ally*, reworked instead of removed** *(Claude, alternative to A3)*. The caster beseeches a **dragon**, or an elemental power, and an Elemental is sent. Payment goes to that dragon's faith.

**B10. *Plane Shift*, *Gate* and *Commune with Nature*** *(follows from Troy)*.
- *Plane Shift* and *Gate* now reach only the kept planes.
- *Plane Shift*'s example of a destination (the Nine Hells) needs replacing.
- *Commune with Nature*'s lists drop Celestials, Fey and Fiends.

## C. Waiting on other brainstorms (not proposed yet)

- ***Reincarnate*** **becomes the phasing spell**, with its d10 table turned into the colour wheel. From the phasing brainstorm, still PROPOSED.
- ***Power Word: Phase*** **(new)**, **_Anchor_ (new)** and ***Change of Heart*** **(new)**. Also from the phasing brainstorm, still PROPOSED.
- **The revival spells** (*Revivify*, *Raise Dead*, *Resurrection*, *True Resurrection*, *Clone*): may change once phasing settles. The **Dead condition** (the spirit "departed for the Outer Planes") needs a new destination either way.
- **The Astral as Azhi's cosmos** *(Troy, PROPOSED)*: if adopted, *Astral Projection* and the Astral spells may only need flavour changes.
- **Pocket dimensions as machine-age technology** *(Troy, PROPOSED)*: if adopted, *Demiplane*, *Rope Trick*, *Magnificent Mansion*, *Maze* and *Imprisonment* may get a flavour note.
- **The 9th-level cap** *(Troy)*: already the rule; it now has an in-world reason (epoch 3). No spell text changes.

## Not changing

- **Black's and Grey's form rituals** cast without Material components. That rule lives in the species chapter, not in the spells.
