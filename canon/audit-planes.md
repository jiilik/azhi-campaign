# Plane Audit

**Status: report only** (Claude, 2026-10-31). This lists everything that depends on planes beyond the Material Plane. It doesn't decide anything; the decisions belong in the planes brainstorm (`canon/brainstorm-epochs.md`).

## Method

- **The SRD:** I searched every SRD 5.2.1 section file in `srd/` for:
  - named planes: Astral, Ethereal, Feywild, Shadowfell, the Elemental Planes and their four names, the Inner, Outer, Upper and Lower Planes, Abyss, Nine Hells, Mechanus, Limbo;
  - general phrases: "another plane", "plane of existence", demiplane;
  - the creature types tied to planes: Celestial, Elemental, Fey, Fiend.
  
  That gave 230 passages. They're grouped below by what each one depends on.
- **Non-SRD subclasses** (§5) are listed by **name only, from memory, unverified**. They're not in the SRD, so they need checking against the books before any player-facing list goes out.

**Troy's working ideas, for reference** (PROPOSED, 2026-10-31):
- **Elemental planes:** fire, water and earth, plus a cold plane of air. All mostly sealed since epoch 3, with imperfect rifts.
- **The Astral:** perhaps the cosmos Azhi wandered.
- **Pocket dimensions:** machine-age technology.
- **The machines' own plane.**
- **No Feywild, heavens or hells** (leaning).

## 8. What this means, in short (observations only)

1. **The Astral and Ethereal** hold up a lot of everyday magic: extradimensional storage, *Blink*, Truesight, ghosts. Troy's "Astral as Azhi's cosmos" idea would keep most of the Astral items working. Nothing yet covers the Ethereal.
2. **Most elemental content survives** if the four Elemental Planes exist.
3. **Fey, celestials and fiends** touch three SRD classes directly: the cleric's and paladin's Channel Divinity wording, and the warlock's only SRD subclass (the Fiend). They also touch about 57 SRD monsters, several common spells (*Find Familiar*, *Find Steed*, the *Protection from Evil and Good* family) and five languages.
4. **The Dead condition** sends spirits to the Outer Planes. Phasing and rebirth will need their own answer to where a spirit goes.
5. **"On the same plane" limits** are harmless if there's effectively one plane.
6. **Undead** (19 SRD monsters) are a separate open question; this audit only notes them.
